Dates matter in every part of the exam. The form in the writing task asks for your *Geburtsdatum*; messages tell you when to come; announcements give the date of an event. Weather is a standard small-talk topic and a favourite of the listening part, where you hear a short forecast.

## The idea

Weather in German is mostly said with **es**: *Es regnet. Es ist kalt.* The *es* does not refer to anything; it is there because a German sentence needs a subject.

Dates need a new kind of number, the **ordinal**: *first, second, third*. German ordinals are made by adding **-te** to small numbers and **-ste** to bigger ones, with three irregular forms to learn.

Then there is one switch to watch. To say what the date *is*, the ordinal ends in **-e**: *Heute ist der dritt**e** Mai.* To say *on* a date, it ends in **-en**: *am dritt**en** Mai.* That **-en** is the dative you met in chapter 16, because *am* is *an dem*.

## Step by step

<Step n="1" title="The weather">

| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Die Sonne scheint. | The sun is shining. |
| Es regnet. | It is raining. |
| Es schneit. | It is snowing. |
| Es ist sonnig. | It is sunny. |
| Es ist bewölkt. | It is cloudy. |
| Es ist windig. | It is windy. |
| Es ist warm / heiß. | It is warm / hot. |
| Es ist kalt / kühl. | It is cold / cool. |
| Es gibt ein Gewitter. | There is a thunderstorm. |

<Example de="Wie ist das Wetter?" en="What is the weather like?" />
<Example de="Das Wetter ist schön. Die Sonne scheint." en="The weather is nice. The sun is shining." />
<Example de="Es regnet und es ist kalt." en="It is raining and it is cold." />
<Example de="Heute sind es zwanzig Grad." en="It is twenty degrees today." />
<Example de="Im Winter schneit es oft." en="In winter it often snows." />

The nouns: *der Regen* (rain), *der Schnee* (snow), *der Wind*, *die Sonne*, *die Wolke* (cloud), *das Gewitter* (thunderstorm), *die Temperatur*, *der Grad* (degree).

Temperatures below zero are **minus**: *minus fünf Grad*.

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="Seasons and months">

| Season | Months |
| --- | --- |
| der Frühling (spring) | März, April, Mai |
| der Sommer (summer) | Juni, Juli, August |
| der Herbst (autumn) | September, Oktober, November |
| der Winter (winter) | Dezember, Januar, Februar |

All months and seasons are masculine, and all take **im**.

<Example de="Januar, Februar, März, April, Mai, Juni." />
<Example de="Juli, August, September, Oktober, November, Dezember." />
<Example de="**Im** Sommer ist es warm." en="In summer it is warm." />
<Example de="**Im** Januar habe ich Geburtstag." en="My birthday is in January." />
<Example de="Wir fahren **im** August in den Urlaub." en="We are going on holiday in August." />

So the time prepositions are now complete: **um** for clock times, **am** for days and dates, **im** for months and seasons.

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="Ordinal numbers">

From 1 to 19, add **-te** to the number. From 20 upwards, add **-ste**.

| | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. **erste** | 2. zweite | 3. **dritte** | 4. vierte |
| 5. fünfte | 6. sechste | 7. **siebte** | 8. **achte** |
| 9. neunte | 10. zehnte | 11. elfte | 12. zwölfte |
| 19. neunzehnte | 20. zwanzig**ste** | 21. einundzwanzig**ste** | 30. dreißig**ste** |

Four are irregular: **erste** (not *einste*), **dritte** (not *dreite*), **siebte** (shortened) and **achte** (only one t).

In writing, an ordinal is shown by a full stop after the figure: **3.** means *third*.

<Example de="der erste, der zweite, der dritte, der vierte." en="the first, the second, the third, the fourth" />
<Example de="der siebte, der achte, der zwanzigste, der einunddreißigste." en="the 7th, the 8th, the 20th, the 31st" />

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="What is the date today?">

<Example de="Der Wievielte ist heute?" en="What is the date today?" />
<Example de="Heute ist **der** dritt**e** Mai." en="Today is the third of May." />
<Example de="Morgen ist **der** einundzwanzigst**e** Juni." en="Tomorrow is the 21st of June." />

The pattern is **der** + ordinal ending in **-e** + month. There is no word for *of*.

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="On a date">

To say *when* something happens, use **am** and the ordinal ending in **-en**.

<Example de="Wann hast du Geburtstag?" en="When is your birthday?" />
<Example de="Ich habe **am** fünft**en** Mai Geburtstag." en="My birthday is on the fifth of May." />
<Example de="Der Kurs beginnt **am** erst**en** September." en="The course begins on the first of September." />
<Example de="Wir kommen **am** zwanzigst**en** Juli." en="We are coming on the twentieth of July." />

A span of dates uses **vom … bis zum …**:

<Example de="Ich bin vom ersten bis zum zehnten August im Urlaub." en="I am on holiday from the first to the tenth of August." />

</Step>

<Step n="6" title="Writing dates and years">

German writes the **day first, then the month, then the year**, separated by full stops.

| Written | Spoken |
| --- | --- |
| 03.05.2026 | der dritte Mai zweitausendsechsundzwanzig |
| 21.06. | der einundzwanzigste Juni |
| Berlin, den 3. Mai 2026 | (at the top of a letter) |

Years up to 1999 are said in hundreds; from 2000 they are said as an ordinary number. There is no *in* before a year.

| Year | Spoken |
| --- | --- |
| 1995 | neunzehnhundertfünfundneunzig |
| 2000 | zweitausend |
| 2024 | zweitausendvierundzwanzig |

<Example de="Ich bin neunzehnhundertfünfundneunzig geboren." en="I was born in 1995." />
<Example de="Ich bin am fünften Mai neunzehnhundertfünfundneunzig geboren." en="I was born on 5 May 1995." />

On a form you write it in figures: **Geburtsdatum: 05.05.1995**.

</Step>

<Step n="7" title="Birthdays and holidays">

<Example de="Herzlichen Glückwunsch zum Geburtstag!" en="Happy birthday!" />
<Example de="Alles Gute!" en="All the best!" />
<Example de="Frohe Weihnachten!" en="Merry Christmas!" />
<Example de="Ein gutes neues Jahr!" en="Happy New Year!" />

| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| der Geburtstag | birthday |
| Weihnachten | Christmas |
| Ostern | Easter |
| Silvester | New Year's Eve |
| der Feiertag | public holiday |
| die Ferien (plural) | school holidays |

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="It is raining." de="Es regnet." note="Both languages use an empty 'it'. German has no '-ing' form: the simple present does the job." />

<Compare en="the 3rd **of** May" de="der 3. Mai" note="No 'of' in a German date, and the ordinal is marked with a full stop." />

<Compare en="**on** 5 May, **in** May, **at** five o'clock" de="**am** 5. Mai, **im** Mai, **um** fünf Uhr" note="Three prepositions in both languages, and they line up neatly: on = am, in = im, at = um." />

<Compare en="05/03/2026 (US: May 3rd)" de="03.05.2026" note="Day before month, as in British English, and with full stops. Getting this the wrong way round on a form is a real mistake." />

<Compare en="I was born **in** 1995." de="Ich bin 1995 geboren." note="No preposition before a year in German." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="Heute ist der **drei** Mai." right="Heute ist der **dritte** Mai." why="Dates use ordinal numbers, and 'third' is the irregular dritte." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich habe **am fünfte** Mai Geburtstag." right="Ich habe **am fünften** Mai Geburtstag." why="After am the ordinal ends in -en." />

<Mistake wrong="**In** Januar ist es kalt." right="**Im** Januar ist es kalt." why="Months and seasons take im, which is in dem." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich bin **in** 1995 geboren." right="Ich bin 1995 geboren." why="German puts no preposition before a year." />

<Mistake wrong="**Es ist** regnet." right="**Es** regnet." why="regnen is a verb of its own. You do not add ist." />

## Summary

<Summary>

| Time | Preposition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| clock time | um | um acht Uhr |
| day, date | am | am Montag, am dritten Mai |
| month, season | im | im Mai, im Sommer |
| year | (none) | 1995 |

| Ordinals | Rule | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–19 | number + **te** | zweite, vierte, zehnte (but **erste, dritte, siebte, achte**) |
| 20+ | number + **ste** | zwanzigste, einunddreißigste |

**The date is:** der dritt**e** Mai. **On the date:** am dritt**en** Mai.

**Weather:** Es regnet. · Es schneit. · Die Sonne scheint. · Es ist warm / kalt / windig / bewölkt.

</Summary>
