*Was sind Sie von Beruf?* is one of the fixed questions of an A1 introduction, and "Beruf" is on the cue card. Job adverts and workplace notices also turn up in the reading exam. Compared with the last few chapters this one is light on grammar: three small rules, and a set of words.

## The idea

There are three things to know about jobs in German, and each differs from English.

**No article.** *Ich bin Lehrer*, not *Ich bin ein Lehrer*. A job is treated as something you are, like a nationality.

**Two forms.** Almost every job has a male and a female form. The female form usually adds **-in**: *Lehrer → Lehrerin*. You use the one that matches the person you are talking about.

**Two verbs.** You can say what you *are* (*Ich bin Köchin*) or what you *work as* (*Ich arbeite als Köchin*). Both are common and mean the same.

Some female forms take an umlaut as well: *Arzt → Ärztin, Koch → Köchin*.

A few are different words: *der Hausmann / die Hausfrau* (house husband / housewife), *der Kaufmann / die Kauffrau* (businessman / businesswoman).

<Step n="2" title="Saying what you do">

<Example de="Was sind Sie von Beruf?" en="What is your job?" />
<Example de="Was machst du beruflich?" en="What do you do for a living?" />
<Example de="Ich bin Ingenieur." en="I am an engineer." />
<Example de="Ich bin Lehrerin." en="I am a teacher." />
<Example de="Ich arbeite **als** Verkäufer." en="I work as a shop assistant." />

No article after *sein* and after *als*.

If you are not working:

<Example de="Ich bin Student." en="I am a student." />
<Example de="Ich bin arbeitslos. Ich suche Arbeit." en="I am unemployed. I am looking for work." />
<Example de="Ich bin Rentner." en="I am retired." />

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="Where you work">

Two prepositions, both with the dative.

| Word | Used with | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **bei** | a company or an employer | Ich arbeite **bei** Siemens. |
| **in** | a place | Ich arbeite **in einem** Büro. |

<Example de="Ich arbeite **bei** einer Bank." en="I work for a bank." />
<Example de="Sie arbeitet **in einem** Krankenhaus." en="She works in a hospital." />
<Example de="Er arbeitet **in einer** Schule." en="He works in a school." />
<Example de="Wir arbeiten **im** Büro." en="We work in the office." />

| Workplace | |
| --- | --- |
| das Büro | office |
| die Firma | company |
| die Fabrik | factory |
| das Geschäft | shop |
| die Werkstatt | workshop |
| die Baustelle | building site |

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="Since when">

Use **seit** with the present tense, as you did for illness.

<Example de="Ich arbeite **seit** zwei Jahren bei dieser Firma." en="I have been working for this company for two years." />
<Example de="Wie lange arbeiten Sie schon hier?" en="How long have you been working here?" />
<Example de="Seit 2022." en="Since 2022." />

</Step>

<Step n="7" title="Reading a job advert">

| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Wir suchen … | We are looking for … |
| ab sofort | starting now |
| die Stelle | position, job |
| die Bewerbung | application |
| die Erfahrung | experience |
| der Führerschein | driving licence |
| Arbeitszeit | working hours |
| … Euro pro Stunde | … euros an hour |

In adverts you will see the letters **(m/w/d)** after a job title: *männlich, weiblich, divers*. It means the job is open to everyone.

<Example de="Wir suchen ab sofort einen Kellner oder eine Kellnerin." en="We are looking for a waiter or waitress, starting now." />
<Example de="Arbeitszeit: Freitag und Samstag, achtzehn bis dreiundzwanzig Uhr." en="Hours: Friday and Saturday, 6 pm to 11 pm." />

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="I am **a** teacher." de="Ich bin Lehrer." note="No article before a job in German." />

<Compare en="She is a teacher. He is a teacher." de="Sie ist Lehrer**in**. Er ist Lehrer." note="German marks the gender of the person in the job title." />

<Compare en="I work **for** a bank." de="Ich arbeite **bei** einer Bank." note="For an employer German uses bei, not für." />

<Compare en="I **have been working** here **for** two years." de="Ich **arbeite seit** zwei Jahren hier." note="Present tense with seit, because you still work there." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="Ich bin **ein** Ingenieur." right="Ich bin Ingenieur." why="Jobs take no article after sein." />

<Mistake wrong="Meine Mutter ist **Lehrer**." right="Meine Mutter ist **Lehrerin**." why="A woman's job takes the female form in -in." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich arbeite **für** Siemens." right="Ich arbeite **bei** Siemens." why="Working for a company is bei." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich arbeite **wie** Koch." right="Ich arbeite **als** Koch." why="'As' in the sense of a role is als. wie means 'like'." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich **habe** zwei Jahre hier **gearbeitet**. (and you still do)" right="Ich **arbeite seit** zwei Jahren hier." why="If you still work there, use the present with seit. The Perfekt would mean you have stopped." />

## Summary

| To say | German |
| --- | --- |
| your job | Ich bin Lehrer / Lehrerin. · Ich arbeite als … |
| your employer | Ich arbeite bei … |
| your workplace | Ich arbeite in einem Büro / in einer Schule. |
