The writing part of *Start Deutsch 1* lasts **20 minutes** and has two tasks. It is the part where preparation pays off most directly, because both tasks follow a fixed pattern and are marked against a fixed list.

## What this part tests

**Teil 1: a form.** You read a few lines about a person and a form with some fields already filled in. Five fields are empty. You find the information in the text and write it into the right field. One point for each correct field.

**Teil 2: a short message.** You get a situation and **three content points** (*Leitpunkte*). You write a short email or note of about **30 words** that deals with all three, with a greeting at the top and a closing at the bottom.

The examiners look at two things in Teil 2. First, **content**: is each of the three points there, and can it be understood? Second, **communication**: does the text work as a message, with a fitting opening and ending and the right choice of *du* or *Sie*? Small grammar and spelling mistakes cost little as long as the meaning is clear. A missing content point costs a lot.

## How to approach it

<Step n="1" title="The form: read the labels first">

Before you read the text, look at the empty fields. Then you know what you are searching for. These are the labels you will meet:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Familienname / Nachname | surname |
| Vorname | first name |
| Straße, Hausnummer | street, house number |
| Postleitzahl (PLZ), Wohnort | postcode, town |
| Geburtsdatum | date of birth |
| Geburtsort | place of birth |
| Staatsangehörigkeit / Nationalität | nationality |
| Familienstand | marital status |
| Beruf | job |
| Telefon | phone number |
| Anzahl der Personen | number of people |
| Anreise / Abreise | arrival / departure |
| Zahlungsweise | method of payment |
| Unterschrift | signature |

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="The form: copy carefully">

The answer is nearly always in the text, word for word. Copy it exactly, including capital letters. Three traps:

- **Numbers written as words.** The text says *zwei Erwachsene und drei Kinder*; the field *Anzahl der Personen* wants **5**.
- **Dates.** Write them the German way: day, month, year. *am 3. Mai* becomes **03.05.**
- **Words that are implied.** If the text says *Er zahlt mit Kreditkarte*, the field *Zahlungsweise* wants **Kreditkarte**. If it says *Sie ist nicht verheiratet*, the field *Familienstand* wants **ledig**.

<Example de="Familienstand: verheiratet, ledig, geschieden." en="Marital status: married, single, divorced." />
<Example de="Zahlungsweise: bar, mit Karte, per Überweisung." en="Payment: cash, by card, by bank transfer." />

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="The message: du or Sie?">

Decide this before you write a word, and then stay with it. The task tells you who you are writing to.

| Writing to | Opening | Pronoun | Closing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a friend | Hallo Anna, / Liebe Anna, / Lieber Tom, | du, dir, dein | Viele Grüße / Liebe Grüße |
| a stranger, an office, a teacher | Sehr geehrte Frau Weber, / Sehr geehrter Herr Weber, / Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren, | Sie, Ihnen, Ihr | Mit freundlichen Grüßen |

**Liebe** goes with a woman's name and **Lieber** with a man's. In the same way, *Sehr geehrte Frau* and *Sehr geehrter Herr*.

After the opening comes a comma, and the first sentence then starts with a **small** letter:

<Example de="Liebe Anna, ich komme am Samstag." en="Dear Anna, I am coming on Saturday." />
<Example de="Sehr geehrte Frau Weber, ich möchte einen Termin." en="Dear Mrs Weber, I would like an appointment." />

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="The message: one sentence for each point">

Do not try to be clever. Take the three points in order and write one or two plain sentences for each. Tick each point off on the paper as you finish it.

Suppose the task is: *You want to visit your friend Jan in Hamburg. Write to him. Why are you writing? When are you coming? Ask where you can sleep.*

<Example de="Lieber Jan," />
<Example de="ich möchte dich im August besuchen." en="I would like to visit you in August. (point 1: why)" />
<Example de="Ich komme am zehnten August mit dem Zug." en="I am coming on 10 August by train. (point 2: when)" />
<Example de="Kann ich bei dir schlafen?" en="Can I sleep at your place? (point 3: where to sleep)" />
<Example de="Viele Grüße" />
<Example de="Maria" />

That is 26 words plus the greeting and the name, and it would score full marks for content.

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="Sentences you can reuse">

| Purpose | Informal | Formal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| thanking | Danke für deine E-Mail. | Vielen Dank für Ihre E-Mail. |
| inviting | Ich möchte dich einladen. | Ich möchte Sie einladen. |
| accepting | Ich komme gern. | Ich komme gern. |
| declining | Ich kann leider nicht kommen. | Ich kann leider nicht kommen. |
| giving a reason | Ich muss arbeiten. / Ich bin krank. | Ich bin leider krank. |
| asking for information | Wann beginnt der Kurs? | Können Sie mir bitte Informationen schicken? |
| asking a favour | Kannst du mir helfen? | Können Sie mir bitte helfen? |
| suggesting a time | Hast du am Samstag Zeit? | Haben Sie am Montag Zeit? |
| ending | Bis bald! | Ich freue mich auf Ihre Antwort. |

</Step>

<Step n="6" title="Check before you hand in">

Leave two minutes. Read your message once and ask:

1. Are **all three points** there?
2. Is there an **opening** and a **closing**, and do they match (*du* with *Viele Grüße*, *Sie* with *Mit freundlichen Grüßen*)?
3. Is the **verb in second place** in each sentence?
4. Does every **noun** have a capital letter?
5. Is my **name** at the bottom?

</Step>

## Summary

<Summary>

| | Teil 1 | Teil 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task | complete 5 fields of a form | write a message of about 30 words |
| Source | a short text about a person | a situation and 3 content points |
| What counts | the right information in the right field | all 3 points, greeting, closing, du or Sie |

**Message skeleton:**

1. *Liebe … / Lieber …,* or *Sehr geehrte Frau … / Sehr geehrter Herr …,*
2. Point 1 · Point 2 · Point 3, one sentence each
3. *Viele Grüße* or *Mit freundlichen Grüßen*
4. Your name

**Time:** about 5 minutes for the form, 12 for the message, 3 to check.

</Summary>
