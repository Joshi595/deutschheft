*Although it was raining, we went out. It was raining. Nevertheless we went out. Despite the rain we went out.* Three sentences, one meaning, three different structures. At B1 you need to be able to say the same thing in more than one way: it makes your writing varied, and the B1 reading and writing tasks reward exactly that. This chapter organises the connectors you know and adds the important new ones: **obwohl**, **trotz**, **da** and **sodass**.

## The idea

Every logical relation in German can be expressed in three ways:

| | Subordinating conjunction (verb last) | Adverb (position 1, verb next) | Preposition (+ noun) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **contrast** | **obwohl** es regnet | Es regnet. **Trotzdem** gehen wir. | **trotz** des Regens |
| **reason** | **weil / da** es regnet | Es regnet. **Deshalb** bleiben wir. | **wegen** des Regens |
| **result** | Es regnet, **sodass** wir bleiben. | Es regnet. **Also** bleiben wir. | – |

Once you know which group a word belongs to, you know its word order. That is the whole trick.

## Step by step

<Step n="1" title="obwohl: although">

*obwohl* introduces a subordinate clause with something that **should** prevent the main action, but does not.

<Example de="**Obwohl** es **regnet**, gehen wir spazieren." en="Although it is raining, we are going for a walk." />
<Example de="Er kam zur Arbeit, **obwohl** er krank **war**." en="He came to work although he was ill." />
<Example de="**Obwohl** sie wenig Zeit **hat**, hilft sie mir immer." en="Although she has little time, she always helps me." />

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="trotzdem and trotz">

The same contrast with an adverb (from A2) and with a preposition (+ genitive, in speech often dative).

<Example de="Es regnet. **Trotzdem gehen** wir spazieren." en="It is raining. We are going for a walk anyway." />
<Example de="**Trotz** des Regens gehen wir spazieren." en="Despite the rain we are going for a walk." />
<Example de="**Trotz** seiner Krankheit kam er zur Arbeit." en="Despite his illness he came to work." />

*obwohl* belongs to the clause with the obstacle; *trotzdem* belongs to the clause with the result.

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="da: since, as">

*da* gives a reason, like *weil*. The difference is one of emphasis: *da* is used for a reason that is **known** or **less important**, and the *da* clause usually comes **first**. It is common in written and formal German.

<Example de="**Da** ich kein Auto **habe**, fahre ich mit dem Bus." en="Since I do not have a car, I take the bus." />
<Example de="**Da** der Kurs ausgebucht **ist**, können wir Sie leider nicht anmelden." en="As the course is fully booked, we unfortunately cannot register you." />

*weil* is the general word, and the only one to answer a *warum* question: *Warum kommst du nicht? – **Weil** ich krank bin.* (not *Da …*)

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="The reason family">

| Type | Word | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| subordinating | weil, da | …, weil ich krank bin. |
| position 0 | denn | …, denn ich bin krank. |
| adverb (position 1) | deshalb, deswegen, darum, daher | Ich bin krank. Deshalb bleibe ich … |
| preposition | wegen + gen. | Wegen meiner Krankheit bleibe ich … |

<Example de="**Wegen** des Streiks fahren heute keine Züge." en="Because of the strike there are no trains today." />

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="sodass and so … dass: with the result">

*sodass* (also *so dass*) introduces a subordinate clause with the **result** or **consequence**.

<Example de="Es hat stark geregnet, **sodass** das Spiel ausfallen **musste**." en="It rained heavily, so that the match had to be cancelled." />
<Example de="Er hat verschlafen, **sodass** er den Zug **verpasst hat**." en="He overslept, so he missed the train." />

With an adjective or adverb that you want to stress, split it: **so** + adjective … **dass**.

<Example de="Ich war **so** müde, **dass** ich sofort eingeschlafen **bin**." en="I was so tired that I fell asleep immediately." />

</Step>

<Step n="6" title="One idea, three structures">

Being able to rephrase is a real B1 skill. Practise with this pattern:

| Structure | Contrast |
| --- | --- |
| obwohl | **Obwohl** das Hotel teuer war, haben wir es gebucht. |
| trotzdem | Das Hotel war teuer. **Trotzdem** haben wir es gebucht. |
| trotz | **Trotz** des hohen Preises haben wir das Hotel gebucht. |

| Structure | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| weil / da | **Da** ich krank war, bin ich zu Hause geblieben. |
| deshalb | Ich war krank. **Deshalb** bin ich zu Hause geblieben. |
| wegen | **Wegen** meiner Krankheit bin ich zu Hause geblieben. |

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="**Although** it is raining …" de="**Obwohl** es **regnet**, …" note="obwohl sends the verb to the end, like weil." />

<Compare en="It is raining. **Nevertheless** we go." de="Es regnet. **Trotzdem gehen wir**." note="trotzdem takes position 1, so the verb follows it directly." />

<Compare en="**Since** I have no car …" de="**Da** ich kein Auto habe, …" note="English 'since' can mean time or reason. German da here means reason only; for time use seit." />

<Compare en="so tired **that** …" de="**so** müde, **dass** …" note="The same pattern in both languages." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="Obwohl es regnet, **wir gehen** spazieren." right="Obwohl es regnet, **gehen wir** spazieren." why="The obwohl clause takes position 1, so the main clause starts with the verb." />

<Mistake wrong="**Trotzdem** es regnet, gehen wir spazieren." right="**Obwohl** es regnet, gehen wir spazieren." why="trotzdem is an adverb, not a conjunction. For a subordinate clause use obwohl." />

<Mistake wrong="Warum kommst du nicht? – **Da** ich krank bin." right="Warum kommst du nicht? – **Weil** ich krank bin." why="Only weil answers a warum question." />

<Mistake wrong="Es hat geregnet, **sodass** das Spiel **musste** ausfallen." right="Es hat geregnet, **sodass** das Spiel ausfallen **musste**." why="sodass sends the conjugated verb to the end." />

<Mistake wrong="**Trotz** es regnet …" right="**Trotz** des Regens …" why="trotz is a preposition and needs a noun, not a clause." />

## Summary

<Summary>

| Relation | Conjunction (verb last) | Adverb (verb next) | Preposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| contrast | obwohl | trotzdem | trotz + gen. |
| reason | weil, da | deshalb, deswegen, darum, daher | wegen + gen. |
| result | sodass, so … dass | also, deshalb | – |

**da** = known reason, usually first, formal. **weil** = general, answers warum.

</Summary>
