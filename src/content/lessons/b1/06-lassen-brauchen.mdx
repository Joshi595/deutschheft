*I am having my hair cut. Let me help you. I left my umbrella on the train. Let's go!* In English these need four different verbs. In German they all use one: **lassen**. It is a very frequent verb, and the meaning *have something done* is one that B1 learners need constantly: at the hairdresser, the garage, the tailor, the office. This chapter also covers **brauchen … zu**, which you will hear whenever someone tells you that you do *not* need to do something.

## The idea

**lassen** is an irregular verb (*du lässt, er lässt; ließ; hat gelassen*). It is used in two ways:

1. **Like a modal verb**, with another verb in the infinitive at the end, without *zu*:
   *Ich **lasse** mein Auto **reparieren**.* I am having my car repaired.
2. **On its own**, with an object:
   *Ich **lasse** das Handy zu Hause.* I am leaving my phone at home.

The meanings that go with each:

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| lassen + infinitive | have something done | Ich lasse mir die Haare schneiden. |
| lassen + person + infinitive | let, allow | Meine Eltern lassen mich allein reisen. |
| lassen + object | leave (somewhere, alone) | Lass mich in Ruhe! |
| Lass uns / Lasst uns + infinitive | let's | Lass uns gehen! |

## Step by step

<Step n="1" title="Having something done">

You do not do it yourself; someone else does it for you. The verb at the end says **what is done**.

<Example de="Ich **lasse** mein Fahrrad **reparieren**." en="I am having my bike repaired." />
<Example de="Wir **lassen** die Wohnung **streichen**." en="We are having the flat painted." />
<Example de="Sie **lässt** sich die Haare **färben**." en="She is having her hair dyed." />

For something done to **your own body**, add a dative reflexive pronoun: *Ich lasse **mir** die Haare schneiden. Er lässt **sich** einen Zahn ziehen.*

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="Letting and allowing">

With a person + infinitive, *lassen* means *let* or *allow*.

<Example de="Meine Eltern **lassen** mich nicht allein in den Urlaub **fahren**." en="My parents do not let me go on holiday alone." />
<Example de="**Lass** mich bitte **ausreden**!" en="Please let me finish speaking!" />
<Example de="Der Lehrer **ließ** uns früher **gehen**." en="The teacher let us go early." />

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="Leaving">

On its own, with an object and a place, *lassen* means *leave* (not taking something with you).

<Example de="Ich habe meinen Schirm im Zug **gelassen**." en="I left my umbrella on the train." />
<Example de="**Lass** die Tür bitte offen." en="Please leave the door open." />
<Example de="**Lass** mich in Ruhe!" en="Leave me alone!" />

Do not confuse with **verlassen** (to leave a place or person): *Er **verlässt** das Haus um acht.*

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="Let's">

*Lass uns* (to one person) and *Lasst uns* (to several) introduce a suggestion. It is a little more emphatic than *Wollen wir …?* or *Gehen wir!*

<Example de="**Lass uns** ins Kino **gehen**!" en="Let's go to the cinema!" />
<Example de="**Lasst uns** anfangen!" en="Let's start!" />

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="The Perfekt: two infinitives">

With another verb, *lassen* behaves like a modal verb in the Perfekt: *haben* + the main verb + **lassen** (infinitive, not *gelassen*).

| Use | Perfekt |
| --- | --- |
| with another verb | Ich **habe** mein Auto **reparieren lassen**. |
| on its own | Ich **habe** den Schirm im Zug **gelassen**. |

<Example de="Wir **haben** die Küche neu **machen lassen**." en="We had the kitchen redone." />
<Example de="Ich **habe** mir die Haare **schneiden lassen**. Gefällt es dir?" en="I have had my hair cut. Do you like it?" />

In a subordinate clause, *haben* comes before the two infinitives: *…, weil ich mein Auto **habe reparieren lassen**.* You will mostly see this in writing.

</Step>

<Step n="6" title="nicht brauchen … zu: not need to">

To say something is **not necessary**, German often uses *nicht brauchen* + **zu** + infinitive. It means the same as *nicht müssen*.

<Example de="Du **brauchst** nicht **zu** kommen. = Du musst nicht kommen." en="You do not need to come." />
<Example de="Sie **brauchen** keinen Termin **zu** machen." en="You do not need to make an appointment." />
<Example de="Du **brauchst** mich nur **anzurufen**." en="You only need to call me." />

*brauchen … zu* is only used with **nicht, kein** or **nur**. For a positive obligation, use *müssen*: *Du musst kommen*, not *Du brauchst zu kommen*.

With separable verbs, *zu* goes between prefix and verb: *an**zu**rufen, ab**zu**holen*.

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="I **am having** my car **repaired**." de="Ich **lasse** mein Auto **reparieren**." note="English uses have + past participle. German uses lassen + infinitive." />

<Compare en="**Let** me help you." de="**Lass** mich dir helfen." note="Same idea and similar structure." />

<Compare en="I **left** my bag on the bus." de="Ich habe meine Tasche im Bus **gelassen**." note="leave something somewhere = lassen. leave a place = verlassen." />

<Compare en="You **don't have to** come." de="Du **brauchst** nicht **zu** kommen." note="English 'don't have to' = German nicht müssen or nicht brauchen zu. Not 'du musst nicht' in the sense of 'must not' – that is du darfst nicht." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="Ich lasse mein Auto **zu reparieren**." right="Ich lasse mein Auto **reparieren**." why="lassen works like a modal verb: infinitive without zu." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich habe mein Auto reparieren **gelassen**." right="Ich habe mein Auto reparieren **lassen**." why="With another verb, the Perfekt uses the infinitive lassen." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich **schneide** mir die Haare beim Friseur." right="Ich **lasse** mir die Haare beim Friseur **schneiden**." why="The hairdresser does it, so lassen." />

<Mistake wrong="Du brauchst nicht **kommen**." right="Du brauchst nicht **zu** kommen." why="In careful German, brauchen takes zu. (Without zu is common in speech.)" />

<Mistake wrong="Du **brauchst** morgen **zu** arbeiten." right="Du **musst** morgen arbeiten." why="brauchen … zu is only used with nicht, kein or nur." />

## Summary

<Summary>

| Meaning | Example | Perfekt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| have done | Ich lasse das Auto reparieren. | habe … reparieren **lassen** |
| let, allow | Lass mich ausreden! | hat mich … ausreden **lassen** |
| leave | Ich lasse das Handy zu Hause. | habe … **gelassen** |
| let's | Lass uns gehen! | – |

**lassen:** ich lasse, du lässt, er lässt · ließ · gelassen

**nicht / kein / nur brauchen + zu + infinitive** = not need to / only need to.

</Summary>
