*Wohnungsbaugesellschaft. Unvergesslich. Erreichbarkeit.* Long German words look frightening, but they are built from parts you mostly know already, following a small number of rules. Knowing those rules does two things for you at B1. In **reading**, you can guess the meaning of new words instead of stopping. In **writing**, you can turn a verb into a noun or an adjective into its opposite, which makes your style more varied. And the endings tell you the **gender** of a noun, often with certainty.

## The idea

German builds words in three ways:

1. **Suffixes** (endings) change the word class: *wohnen* → *die Wohn**ung***, *frei* → *die Frei**heit***, *essen* → *ess**bar***.
2. **Prefixes** (beginnings) change the meaning: *freundlich* → ***un**freundlich*, *kaufen* → ***ver**kaufen*.
3. **Compounds** put two or more words together: *die Haus**tür***, *der Arbeit**s**platz*.

Two facts make this predictable:

- Many **suffixes fix the gender**: every noun in *-ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft* is feminine.
- In a compound, the **last word** decides the gender and the basic meaning: *die Tür* → ***die*** *Haustür* (a kind of door).

## Step by step

<Step n="1" title="Nouns from verbs">

| Pattern | Gender | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| stem + **-ung** | die | wohnen → die Wohnung, erklären → die Erklärung |
| infinitive as noun | das | essen → das Essen, lernen → das Lernen |
| stem alone | usually der | besuchen → der Besuch, anrufen → der Anruf |
| stem + **-er** (person, device) | der | lehren → der Lehrer, drucken → der Drucker |

<Example de="Vielen Dank für die **Einladung**!" en="Thank you very much for the invitation!" />
<Example de="Das **Lernen** macht mir Spaß." en="I enjoy learning." />

Female persons add **-in**: *der Lehrer → die Lehrer**in***, plural *-innen*.

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="Nouns from adjectives">

| Suffix | Gender | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **-heit** | die | frei → die Freiheit, krank → die Krankheit |
| **-keit** (after -ig, -lich, -bar, -sam) | die | möglich → die Möglichkeit, freundlich → die Freundlichkeit |
| **-igkeit** | die | müde → die Müdigkeit, schnell → die Schnelligkeit |

<Example de="Die **Freundlichkeit** der Leute hat mich überrascht." en="The friendliness of the people surprised me." />
<Example de="Gibt es eine **Möglichkeit**, den Termin zu verschieben?" en="Is there any possibility of postponing the appointment?" />

Other feminine endings: **-schaft** (die Freundschaft), **-ion** (die Information), **-tät** (die Qualität), **-ie** (die Fotografie). Neuter: **-chen, -lein** (das Mädchen), **-ment** (das Dokument), **-um** (das Museum).

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="Adjectives from other words">

| Suffix | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **-bar** | can be done | essbar (edible), machbar (feasible), erreichbar (reachable) |
| **-los** | without | arbeitslos, kostenlos, hoffnungslos |
| **-lich** | like, relating to | freundlich, täglich, beruflich |
| **-ig** | having, like | sonnig, lustig, schmutzig |
| **-isch** | from, typical of | italienisch, praktisch, typisch |
| **-voll** | full of | wertvoll, sinnvoll |

<Example de="Der Eintritt ist **kostenlos**." en="Admission is free." />
<Example de="Ich bin ab 9 Uhr telefonisch **erreichbar**." en="I can be reached by phone from 9 o'clock." />

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="The prefix un-">

*un-* makes an adjective negative, like English *un-* or *in-*.

| Positive | Negative |
| --- | --- |
| freundlich | **un**freundlich |
| möglich | **un**möglich |
| wichtig | **un**wichtig |
| zufrieden | **un**zufrieden |
| bekannt | **un**bekannt |

<Example de="Das ist **unmöglich**!" en="That is impossible!" />
<Example de="Viele Kunden waren mit dem Service **unzufrieden**." en="Many customers were dissatisfied with the service." />

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="Compound nouns">

The **last** part is the main word: it gives the gender and the plural. The first parts describe it.

| Compound | Parts | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **die** Haustür | das Haus + **die** Tür | front door |
| **der** Kühlschrank | kühlen + **der** Schrank | fridge |
| **das** Fahrrad | fahren + **das** Rad | bicycle |
| **die** Arbeit**s**zeit | die Arbeit + **die** Zeit | working hours |

Often a linking **-s-** appears between the parts, especially after *-ung, -heit, -keit, -ion, -tät* and after *Arbeit, Geburt, Liebe*: *Wohnung**s**suche, Geburt**s**tag, Liebe**s**brief*.

<Example de="Ich bin gerade auf **Wohnungssuche**." en="I am looking for a flat at the moment." />
<Example de="Unsere **Arbeitszeiten** sind sehr flexibel." en="Our working hours are very flexible." />

</Step>

<Step n="6" title="Verb prefixes">

Inseparable prefixes change a verb's meaning. You cannot always predict it, but some patterns help.

| Prefix | Typical meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ver-** | wrong, away, change | sich verlaufen (get lost), verkaufen (sell) |
| **be-** | makes a verb take a direct object | antworten auf → beantworten (eine Frage) |
| **ent-** | away, removal | entfernen (remove), entdecken (discover) |
| **zer-** | to pieces | zerstören (destroy), zerbrechen (break apart) |
| **er-** | achieving a result | erreichen (reach), erfinden (invent) |

<Example de="Bitte **beantworten** Sie alle Fragen." en="Please answer all the questions." />

</Step>

<Step n="7" title="Reading long words">

To understand a new long word, cut it from the **right**: the last part is what the thing *is*.

*Wohnungsbaugesellschaft* = Gesellschaft (company) + Bau (building) + Wohnung (flats) → a housing construction company.

<Example de="Die **Kinderbetreuungskosten** sind gestiegen. → Kosten (costs) + Betreuung (care) + Kinder" en="The costs of childcare have risen." />

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="free → free**dom**, friendly → friendli**ness**" de="frei → Frei**heit**, freundlich → Freundlich**keit**" note="English -dom, -ness, -ity often match German -heit and -keit." />

<Compare en="eat**able** / unemploy**ed** / hope**less**" de="ess**bar** / arbeits**los** / hoffnungs**los**" note="-bar = -able, -los = -less." />

<Compare en="front door, working hours" de="Haustür, Arbeitszeiten" note="English writes compounds as separate words. German writes them as one word." />

<Compare en="**un**friendly" de="**un**freundlich" note="Same prefix, same meaning." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="**der** Wohnung" right="**die** Wohnung" why="All nouns in -ung are feminine." />

<Mistake wrong="**das** Haustür" right="**die** Haustür" why="The last part decides the gender: die Tür." />

<Mistake wrong="Arbeitzeit" right="Arbeit**s**zeit" why="Arbeit takes a linking -s- in compounds." />

<Mistake wrong="die Freund**heit**" right="die Freundlich**keit**" why="After -lich, the suffix is -keit." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich antworte die Frage." right="Ich **be**antworte die Frage. / Ich antworte **auf** die Frage." why="be- makes antworten take a direct object." />

## Summary

<Summary>

| Ending | Gender / class | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft, -ion, -tät | **die** | Wohnung, Freiheit, Möglichkeit |
| -chen, -lein, -ment, -um, infinitive | **das** | Mädchen, Dokument, das Essen |
| -er (person), verb stem | **der** | Lehrer, Besuch |
| -bar, -los, -lich, -ig, -isch, -voll | adjective | machbar, kostenlos |
| un- | opposite | unmöglich |

**Compounds:** the last part decides gender and meaning · linking -s- after -ung, -heit, -keit, Arbeit.

**Long words:** read from the right.

</Summary>
