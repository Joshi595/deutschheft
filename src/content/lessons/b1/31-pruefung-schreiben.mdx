The writing module of the Goethe-Zertifikat B1 lasts **60 minutes** and has **three tasks**. They are deliberately different: one **informal**, one **opinion**, one **formal**. Examiners mark four things: whether you **cover the task** (all content points), whether your text is **coherent** (connectors, structure), whether your **vocabulary** is varied enough, and whether your **grammar** is accurate enough for B1. This chapter gives you a plan for each task.

## What this part tests

| Part | Text | Reader | Length | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Teil 1** | email or message to a friend | du | about 80 words, 3 points | 20 min |
| **Teil 2** | comment in an online forum on a topic, often reacting to someone's statement | everyone | about 80 words | 25 min |
| **Teil 3** | short email to a teacher, boss, landlord, office | Sie | about 40 words | 15 min |

The number of words is a guide. Much less risks missing content; much more risks errors and costs time.

## How to approach it

<Step n="1" title="Teil 1: the informal email">

You get a situation (*Sie waren letztes Wochenende auf einer Hochzeit …*) and three points, often: **describe**, **give a reason**, **suggest or ask**. Write a short paragraph for each point. Start and end in the informal register.

<Example de="Liebe Sophie, stell dir vor: Letztes Wochenende war ich auf der Hochzeit meines Cousins in Italien! …" />

Useful structure: greeting → a sentence of contact (*Wie geht es dir?*) → point 1 → point 2 → point 3 → closing (*Ich freue mich auf deine Antwort! Liebe Grüße*).

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="Teil 2: the opinion post">

You read a short statement from a forum or a TV programme guest book, for example: *„Ich finde, Kinder sollten kein Smartphone haben." (Paul)*. You write your opinion. A clear plan:

1. Refer to the statement: *Paul schreibt, dass … Ich sehe das anders / genauso.*
2. Your opinion with a reason: *Meiner Meinung nach …, weil …*
3. An example or your experience: *Zum Beispiel … / Ich selbst …*
4. The other side: *Zwar …, aber … / Andererseits …*
5. Conclusion: *Deshalb finde ich …*

<Example de="Paul meint, dass Kinder kein Smartphone haben sollten. Ich sehe das ein bisschen anders." />

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="Teil 3: the formal message">

Short and formal: cancel an appointment, apologise, ask for something. Usually three points, often: **apologise / inform**, **reason**, **suggestion or request**.

<Example de="Sehr geehrte Frau Neumann, leider kann ich am Freitag nicht zum Kurs kommen, da ich einen wichtigen Arzttermin habe. Könnten Sie mir bitte die Unterlagen per E-Mail schicken? Mit freundlichen Grüßen, Ali Demir" />

That is about 40 words, formal from start to end.

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="Connectors that score points">

Examiners reward texts that are connected. Use a range:

| Function | Connectors |
| --- | --- |
| reason | weil, da, denn, deshalb |
| contrast | aber, trotzdem, obwohl, zwar … aber, einerseits … andererseits |
| addition | außerdem, auch, nicht nur … sondern auch |
| time | zuerst, danach, als, nachdem, später |
| conclusion | deshalb, also, zusammenfassend |

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="The final check">

Keep five minutes at the end of the hour. For each text ask:

1. **Content:** are all points there?
2. **Register:** du or Sie, consistently? Matching greeting and closing?
3. **Verb position:** second in main clauses, last after weil, dass, wenn, obwohl?
4. **Endings:** articles and adjectives after prepositions?
5. **Capital letters** on nouns and on Sie / Ihnen?

</Step>

## Summary

<Summary>

| | Teil 1 | Teil 2 | Teil 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Text | informal email | opinion post | formal message |
| Register | du | neutral | Sie |
| Length | ~80 words | ~80 words | ~40 words |
| Time | 20 min | 25 min | 15 min |
| Plan | greeting → 3 points → closing | refer → opinion + reason → example → other side → conclusion | greeting → inform → reason → request → closing |

**Always:** all points, consistent register, a range of connectors, five minutes to check.

</Summary>
