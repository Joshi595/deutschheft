At A2 you learned to recognise the genitive and to say *das Auto von meinem Bruder*. At B1 you need the genitive **actively**, especially in writing: *während des Urlaubs, wegen des schlechten Wetters, die Meinung meines Kollegen*. Formal emails, the opinion texts in the exam and almost every newspaper article use it. This chapter also deals with a small group of masculine nouns that behave oddly in every case: *der Kollege, den Kollege**n**, dem Kollege**n**, des Kollege**n***.

## The idea

The genitive has two main jobs:

1. **Possession or belonging** between two nouns: *das Ende **des Films***, *die Meinung **meiner Kollegin***.
2. **After certain prepositions**: *wegen **des Regens***, *während **der Woche***.

The genitive endings follow the familiar pattern: if the article shows the case clearly, the adjective takes **-en**.

| | m | f | n | pl |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| definite | **des** neu**en** Film**s** | **der** neu**en** Stadt | **des** neu**en** Haus**es** | **der** neu**en** Häuser |
| indefinite | **eines** neu**en** Film**s** | **einer** neu**en** Stadt | **eines** neu**en** Haus**es** | – |

Masculine and neuter **nouns** add **-s** or **-es**. Feminine and plural nouns do not change.

## Step by step

<Step n="1" title="-s or -es?">

| Ending | When | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **-es** | one-syllable nouns, and nouns ending in -s, -ß, -x, -z | des Mann**es**, des Kind**es**, des Haus**es**, des Fuß**es** |
| **-s** | longer nouns | des Lehrer**s**, des Wagen**s**, des Computer**s** |

<Example de="Am Ende **des Films** haben alle geweint." en="At the end of the film everyone cried." />
<Example de="Die Fenster **des Hauses** sind sehr alt." en="The windows of the house are very old." />
<Example de="Das ist die Meinung **meiner Kollegin**." en="That is my colleague's opinion." />

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="Adjectives in the genitive">

After an article, the adjective is always **-en**. Without an article (rare, mostly in fixed phrases), it is -en for masculine and neuter, -er for feminine and plural.

<Example de="Wegen **des schlechten** Wetters fällt das Fest aus." en="Because of the bad weather the festival is cancelled." />
<Example de="Die Preise **der neuen** Wohnungen sind sehr hoch." en="The prices of the new flats are very high." />
<Example de="Er trinkt eine Tasse **heißen** Tees. (rare, formal)" en="He is drinking a cup of hot tea." />

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="Genitive prepositions">

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **wegen** | because of | wegen des Streiks |
| **trotz** | despite | trotz der Kälte |
| **während** | during | während des Urlaubs |
| **(an)statt** | instead of | statt eines Autos |
| **innerhalb** | within | innerhalb einer Woche |
| **außerhalb** | outside | außerhalb der Stadt |
| **aufgrund** | due to | aufgrund der hohen Kosten |

<Example de="**Während des Urlaubs** habe ich keine E-Mails gelesen." en="During the holiday I did not read any emails." />
<Example de="Bitte antworten Sie **innerhalb einer Woche**." en="Please reply within a week." />
<Example de="Wir wohnen **außerhalb der Stadt**." en="We live outside the town." />

In speech, *wegen*, *trotz* and *während* are often used with the dative (*wegen dem Wetter*). In writing and in the exam, use the genitive.

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="Genitive or von?">

| Style | Example |
| --- | --- |
| written, formal | das Haus **meiner Eltern** |
| spoken, informal | das Haus **von meinen Eltern** |
| always von | without an article: der Preis **von Brot** · with names ending in -s: das Auto **von Klaus** |

<Example de="Die Ergebnisse **der Studie** zeigen, dass … (written)" en="The results of the study show that …" />

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="The n-declension">

A group of **masculine** nouns adds **-(e)n** in every case except the nominative singular. Most of them refer to **people or animals** and end in **-e**, or come from Latin or Greek with endings like **-ent, -ant, -ist, -at, -oge**.

| | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nom. | der Kollege | die Kollegen |
| Akk. | den Kollege**n** | die Kollegen |
| Dat. | dem Kollege**n** | den Kollegen |
| Gen. | des Kollege**n** | der Kollegen |

| Group | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| masculine in **-e** | der Kollege, Junge, Kunde, Neffe, Name*, Löwe, Affe |
| **-ent, -ant** | der Student, Präsident, Praktikant, Elefant |
| **-ist, -at, -oge** | der Tourist, Polizist, Journalist, Kandidat, Soldat, Biologe |
| others | der Mensch, Herr (Herr**n**, Plural Herr**en**), Nachbar, Bauer |

<Example de="Ich habe **den Kunden** angerufen." en="I called the customer." />
<Example de="Kannst du **dem Jungen** helfen?" en="Can you help the boy?" />
<Example de="Sehr geehrter **Herr** Müller … Ich habe mit **Herrn** Müller gesprochen." en="Dear Mr Müller … I spoke to Mr Müller." />

*der Name* adds -s in the genitive as well: *des Name**ns***. The same for *der Gedanke* and *der Buchstabe*.

</Step>

<Step n="6" title="Why it matters">

Mistakes with the n-declension are very noticeable to examiners, especially in letters: **Herrn** Müller, not *Herr Müller*, after *an, für, mit*.

<Example de="Bitte geben Sie das Paket **Herrn** Weber." en="Please give the parcel to Mr Weber." />

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="the end **of the film**" de="das Ende **des Films**" note="English 'of the' = German genitive article, and the noun takes -s." />

<Compare en="**during** the holiday" de="**während des** Urlaubs" note="In writing, während takes the genitive." />

<Compare en="I called **the customer**." de="Ich habe **den Kunden** angerufen." note="Some masculine nouns add -n in the accusative, dative and genitive. English has nothing like it." />

<Compare en="I spoke to **Mr** Müller." de="Ich habe mit **Herrn** Müller gesprochen." note="Herr is an n-noun: Herrn in every case except the nominative." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="das Ende **des Film**" right="das Ende **des Films**" why="Masculine and neuter nouns add -s in the genitive." />

<Mistake wrong="wegen **des schlechtes** Wetters" right="wegen **des schlechten** Wetters" why="After des, the adjective is -en." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich habe **den Kollege** gefragt." right="Ich habe **den Kollegen** gefragt." why="Kollege is an n-noun: -n in the accusative." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich habe mit **Herr** Müller gesprochen." right="Ich habe mit **Herrn** Müller gesprochen." why="Herr is an n-noun: Herrn after mit (dative)." />

<Mistake wrong="die Meinung **der Kollegins**" right="die Meinung **der Kollegin**" why="Feminine nouns never add -s in the genitive." />

## Summary

<Summary>

| Genitive | m | f | n | pl |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| article + adj. | des neuen …(e)s | der neuen | des neuen …(e)s | der neuen |

**Prepositions + genitive:** wegen · trotz · während · statt · innerhalb · außerhalb · aufgrund

**n-declension (masculine):** -(e)n in every case except nominative singular. *Kollege, Kunde, Junge, Mensch, Herr, Nachbar, Student, Tourist, Polizist …* · *des Namens*

**Style:** genitive in writing; von + dative in speech.

</Summary>
