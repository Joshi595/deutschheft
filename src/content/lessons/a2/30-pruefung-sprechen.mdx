The speaking module of the Goethe-Zertifikat A2 takes about **15 minutes** and you do it **in a pair** with another candidate, in front of two examiners. You get a few minutes to prepare, without a dictionary. Most of the time you talk to your partner, not to the examiners. That makes it feel more like a real conversation, and it means that **reacting** to your partner is part of what is marked.

## What this part tests

| Part | Task | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Teil 1** | **Fragen zur Person.** Each of you draws four cards with a question word and a topic, e.g. *Geburtstag? Wann?* You ask your partner a question from each card, and answer theirs. | about 3–4 minutes |
| **Teil 2** | **Über das eigene Leben erzählen.** You get a card with a topic in the middle (e.g. *Wohnen*, *Freizeit*, *Essen*) and four questions around it. You talk about your own life for about a minute, then the examiner asks one or two more questions. | about 3–4 minutes per person |
| **Teil 3** | **Etwas gemeinsam planen.** You both get a diary page for the same day with different appointments. You must find a time when you are both free to do something together, e.g. visit a friend in hospital or buy a present. | about 3–4 minutes |

The examiners mark whether you **complete the task**, how **fluently** you speak, the **range and correctness** of your language, and your **pronunciation**. Small mistakes are fine. Long silences and one-word answers are not.

## How to approach it

<Step n="1" title="Teil 1: turn the card into a real question">

The card shows a topic and a hint. Make a complete question with a verb.

| Card | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Sprachen? | Welche Sprachen sprichst du? |
| Wohnort? | Wo wohnst du? |
| Geburtstag? | Wann hast du Geburtstag? |
| Lieblingsessen? | Was isst du am liebsten? |
| Sport? | Machst du gern Sport? |

Answer your partner in a full sentence and add one detail: *Ich wohne in Köln, **seit zwei Jahren**, in einer WG.*

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="Teil 2: answer the four questions in order">

A card might look like this: in the middle *Freizeit*, around it *Was? – Mit wem? – Wo? – Wann?*. Go round the card and say two or three sentences for each question. That is your minute.

<Example de="In meiner Freizeit spiele ich gern Volleyball. Ich spiele meistens mit Kollegen aus meiner Firma. Wir trainieren in einer Halle in der Nähe vom Bahnhof, immer am Mittwochabend. Am Wochenende gehe ich lieber wandern, weil ich dann draußen sein möchte." en="In my free time I like playing volleyball. I mostly play with colleagues from my company. We train in a sports hall near the station, always on Wednesday evenings. At the weekend I prefer hiking, because then I want to be outside." />

Use time words (*meistens, immer, am Wochenende*), a reason with *weil* or *denn*, and if possible a Perfekt: *Letztes Jahr habe ich …*. That shows the range the examiners are looking for.

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="Teil 3: suggest, react, decide">

You have a diary page; your partner has a different one. Ask, suggest, explain why a time does not work, and agree. The phrases matter more than the plan.

| Function | Phrases |
| --- | --- |
| suggest | Wann hast du Zeit? · Wie wäre es um …? · Können wir um … ? · Hast du um … Zeit? |
| say no with a reason | Um zehn kann ich leider nicht, da habe ich … · Das geht nicht, weil … |
| say yes | Ja, das passt. · Gute Idee! · Einverstanden. |
| decide | Also, dann treffen wir uns um … am / vor … |

<Example de="– Wie wäre es um zehn Uhr? – Um zehn kann ich leider nicht, da muss ich zum Arzt. Hast du um zwölf Zeit? – Um zwölf esse ich mit meiner Schwester. Aber um zwei passt es. – Gut, dann treffen wir uns um zwei vor dem Krankenhaus." en="– How about ten o'clock? – I can't at ten, I have to go to the doctor's. Are you free at twelve? – At twelve I am having lunch with my sister. But two works. – Good, let's meet at two in front of the hospital." />

Do not agree on the first suggestion if your diary says no. Talking through two or three times is the task.

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="When you do not know a word">

Do not go silent. Describe it, use a simpler word, or ask.

| Situation | Phrase |
| --- | --- |
| a word is missing | Wie sagt man …? · Das ist so ein Ding für … |
| you did not understand | Wie bitte? · Kannst du das noch einmal sagen? |
| you need a second | Moment … · Also … · Ich glaube … |

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="Practise with a timer">

Record yourself on your phone doing Teil 2 with a topic card: *Wohnen, Arbeit, Essen, Urlaub, Familie, Freizeit*. Aim for one minute without stopping. Listen back once and note one thing to improve. Six topics, five minutes each, is half an hour of the most useful preparation there is.

</Step>

## Summary

<Summary>

| Part | Task | Key to success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | questions from word cards | full question with a verb; answer + one detail |
| 2 | talk about your life | go round the four questions; weil, Perfekt, time words |
| 3 | plan something together | suggest, refuse with a reason, agree, sum up |

**Keep talking:** Wie sagt man …? · Moment … · Kannst du das wiederholen?

**Planning phrases:** Wie wäre es um …? · Da kann ich leider nicht, weil … · Das passt. · Dann treffen wir uns …

</Summary>
