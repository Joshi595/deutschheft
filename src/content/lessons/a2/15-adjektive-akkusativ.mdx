*Ich suche **einen** warm**en** Mantel. Ich nehme **den** blau**en**. Ich habe **eine** neu**e** Jacke gekauft.* You talk about things you want, buy, wear and have all the time, and these things are almost always in the accusative. So this is the adjective case you will use most. And it is easier than it looks: compared with the nominative from the last chapter, **only the masculine changes**.

## The idea

Recall the accusative articles from A1. Only the masculine is different from the nominative:

| | masculine | feminine | neuter | plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| nominative | der / ein | die / eine | das / ein | die / meine |
| accusative | **den / einen** | die / eine | das / ein | die / meine |

The adjective follows the same pattern. Feminine, neuter and plural endings are **exactly as in the nominative**. In the masculine, the article becomes **den** or **einen**, and the adjective always becomes **-en**.

*Der neu**e** Tisch ist schön. → Ich kaufe **den** neu**en** Tisch.*
*Ein neu**er** Tisch ist teuer. → Ich kaufe **einen** neu**en** Tisch.*

The "show it once" idea still works: *den* and *einen* already end in **-n**, and the adjective echoes them with **-en**. Without an article, the adjective takes the article's ending itself: *Ich trinke gern **heiß*en* Tee**.*

## Step by step

<Step n="1" title="After den, die, das">

| | masculine | feminine | neuter | plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| accusative | den neu**en** Tisch | die neu**e** Lampe | das neu**e** Bett | die neu**en** Stühle |

<Example de="Ich nehme **den blauen** Pullover." en="I will take the blue jumper." />
<Example de="Kennst du **die neue** Kollegin?" en="Do you know the new colleague?" />
<Example de="Wir kaufen **das große** Sofa." en="We are buying the big sofa." />
<Example de="Ich mag **die alten** Häuser in der Altstadt." en="I like the old houses in the old town." />

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="After einen, eine, ein">

| | masculine | feminine | neuter | plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| accusative | einen neu**en** Tisch | eine neu**e** Lampe | ein neu**es** Bett | keine neu**en** Stühle |

<Example de="Ich suche **einen warmen** Mantel." en="I am looking for a warm coat." />
<Example de="Sie hat **eine tolle** Idee." en="She has a great idea." />
<Example de="Wir haben **ein kleines** Haus gekauft." en="We have bought a small house." />
<Example de="Ich habe **keine sauberen** Socken mehr." en="I have no clean socks left." />

Notice the neuter: *ein* still has no ending in the accusative, so the adjective still shows **-es**.

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="No article">

| | masculine | feminine | neuter | plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| accusative | heiß**en** Tee | kalt**e** Milch | frisch**es** Brot | rot**e** Äpfel |

<Example de="Ich trinke gern **schwarzen** Kaffee." en="I like drinking black coffee." />
<Example de="Wir brauchen **frisches** Brot und **rote** Äpfel." en="We need fresh bread and red apples." />

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="Which noun is accusative?">

The accusative is the direct object: after *haben, brauchen, kaufen, suchen, nehmen, möchten, mögen, finden, sehen, tragen, es gibt*. It also follows the accusative prepositions **für, ohne, durch, gegen, um** and the two-way prepositions with *wohin*.

<Example de="Es gibt **einen schönen** Park in der Nähe." en="There is a nice park nearby." />
<Example de="Das Geschenk ist für **meinen kleinen** Bruder." en="The present is for my little brother." />
<Example de="Ich lege das Buch auf **den runden** Tisch." en="I put the book on the round table." />

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="Asking which one: welcher? and was für ein?">

| Question | Answer | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Welchen** Mantel nehmen Sie? | **Den** schwarz**en**. | Which one (of these)? |
| **Was für einen** Mantel suchen Sie? | **Einen** warm**en**. | What kind of? |

<Example de="**Welche** Jacke gefällt dir? – **Die grüne**." en="Which jacket do you like? – The green one." />
<Example de="**Was für ein** Auto hast du? – **Ein kleines**." en="What kind of car do you have? – A small one." />

The answer can drop the noun, and the adjective keeps its ending: *Den schwarzen. Ein kleines.*

</Step>

<Step n="6" title="Colours and clothes">

Colours are adjectives and follow every rule in this chapter: *rot, blau, grün, gelb, schwarz, weiß, grau, braun*. Only *rosa, lila, orange* and *beige* never take an ending in careful German.

<Example de="Er trägt **einen grauen** Anzug und **eine blaue** Krawatte." en="He is wearing a grey suit and a blue tie." />
<Example de="Ich möchte **das rosa** Kleid anprobieren." en="I would like to try on the pink dress." />

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="I am looking for **a warm** coat." de="Ich suche **einen warmen** Mantel." note="Both the article and the adjective change in the masculine accusative." />

<Compare en="I will take **the blue one**." de="Ich nehme **den blauen**." note="English adds 'one'. German simply leaves out the noun and keeps the ending." />

<Compare en="**What kind of** car do you have?" de="**Was für ein** Auto hast du?" note="'What kind of' is was für ein. Its ein changes like the normal article: was für einen Mantel." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="Ich suche einen **warmer** Mantel." right="Ich suche einen **warmen** Mantel." why="In the masculine accusative the adjective is always -en." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich suche **ein warmen** Mantel." right="Ich suche **einen warmen** Mantel." why="The article changes too: ein becomes einen." />

<Mistake wrong="Wir haben ein **kleine** Haus." right="Wir haben ein **kleines** Haus." why="das Haus is neuter and ein shows nothing, so the adjective takes -es." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich nehme **die blauen** Pullover. (one jumper)" right="Ich nehme **den blauen** Pullover." why="der Pullover is masculine singular: den blauen. die blauen is plural." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich trinke gern **schwarzer** Kaffee." right="Ich trinke gern **schwarzen** Kaffee." why="Kaffee is the object, so masculine accusative: -en." />

## Summary

<Summary>

| Accusative | masculine | feminine | neuter | plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| den / diesen | den neu**en** | die neu**e** | das neu**e** | die neu**en** |
| einen / keinen / meinen | einen neu**en** | eine neu**e** | ein neu**es** | meine neu**en** |
| no article | neu**en** | neu**e** | neu**es** | neu**e** |

**Only the masculine differs from the nominative:** -en after den and einen.

**Questions:** welcher? (which one) · was für ein? (what kind of)

</Summary>
