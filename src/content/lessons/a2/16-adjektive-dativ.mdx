*Ich wohne in **einer** klein**en** Stadt. Ich fahre mit **dem** neu**en** Fahrrad. Ich spreche mit **meinen** alt**en** Freunden.* After two chapters of thinking about endings, here is a rest: in the dative, after an article, the adjective **always ends in -en**. Masculine, feminine, neuter, plural, definite, indefinite: all -en. This chapter shows that, deals with the one exception (no article), and then puts all three cases into one table you can revise from.

## The idea

Look at the dative articles from chapter 10: **dem, der, dem, den** and **einem, einer, einem, keinen**. Every one of them already ends in a clear signal: **-m**, **-r** or **-n**. The case and gender are fully visible in the article, so the adjective has nothing left to show. It takes the weak ending, and in the dative the weak ending is **-en** everywhere.

| | masculine | feminine | neuter | plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| definite | dem neu**en** Tisch | der neu**en** Lampe | dem neu**en** Bett | den neu**en** Stühlen |
| indefinite | einem neu**en** Tisch | einer neu**en** Lampe | einem neu**en** Bett | meinen neu**en** Stühlen |

Only when there is **no article** does the adjective have to carry the signal itself: *mit frisch**em** Brot, mit kalt**er** Milch*. That follows exactly the same idea as in the nominative and accusative.

## Step by step

<Step n="1" title="After an article: -en">

<Example de="Ich wohne in **einer kleinen** Stadt." en="I live in a small town." />
<Example de="Er fährt mit **dem neuen** Auto zur Arbeit." en="He drives to work in the new car." />
<Example de="Wir sprechen mit **unseren alten** Nachbarn." en="We are talking to our old neighbours." />
<Example de="Das Kind spielt mit **einem roten** Ball." en="The child is playing with a red ball." />

Remember the plural noun takes **-n** too: *mit den neu**en** Nachbar**n***.

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="When is it dative?">

You know the triggers from chapter 10 and 11:

- after **aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu, gegenüber**
- after the two-way prepositions with **wo?**
- for the person after **geben, schenken, helfen, gefallen** and the other dative verbs

<Example de="Das Bild hängt an **der weißen** Wand." en="The picture hangs on the white wall." />
<Example de="Ich helfe **meinem kleinen** Bruder bei den Hausaufgaben." en="I help my little brother with his homework." />
<Example de="Die Jacke gefällt **der jungen** Frau." en="The young woman likes the jacket." />

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="No article: the adjective shows the case">

Without an article, the adjective takes the ending of the dative article: **-em** (dem), **-er** (der), **-em** (dem), **-en** (den).

| masculine | feminine | neuter | plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| mit heiß**em** Tee | mit kalt**er** Milch | mit frisch**em** Brot | mit nett**en** Leuten |

<Example de="Ich trinke Tee **mit warmer** Milch." en="I drink tea with warm milk." />
<Example de="Ein Haus **mit großem** Garten." en="A house with a big garden." />
<Example de="Ich koche gern **mit frischem** Gemüse." en="I like cooking with fresh vegetables." />

Small adverts use this all the time: *Zimmer mit eigenem Bad. Wohnung mit großem Balkon.*

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="The whole system in one table">

Here are the three cases together. The idea is the same throughout: **if the article shows the case, the adjective takes -e or -en; if not, the adjective shows it.**

**After der, die, das (and dieser, jeder, welcher):**

| | m | f | n | pl |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nom. | der neu**e** | die neu**e** | das neu**e** | die neu**en** |
| Akk. | den neu**en** | die neu**e** | das neu**e** | die neu**en** |
| Dat. | dem neu**en** | der neu**en** | dem neu**en** | den neu**en** |

**After ein, kein, mein:**

| | m | f | n | pl |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nom. | ein neu**er** | eine neu**e** | ein neu**es** | meine neu**en** |
| Akk. | einen neu**en** | eine neu**e** | ein neu**es** | meine neu**en** |
| Dat. | einem neu**en** | einer neu**en** | einem neu**en** | meinen neu**en** |

A pattern to see: after the definite article there are only five **-e** endings (nominative singular, plus feminine and neuter accusative). **Everything else is -en.**

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="A strategy for speaking">

You do not have time to look at tables when you speak. Two habits help.

1. **Learn noun groups, not single words.** Say *mit dem neuen Auto, in einer kleinen Stadt, einen guten Freund* as whole chunks until they sound right.
2. **When in doubt, -en.** It is the most common ending by far. It will be wrong sometimes, but less often than any other guess.

<Example de="Ich war gestern **bei einem guten** Freund." en="Yesterday I was at a good friend's place." />
<Example de="Sie arbeitet **in einem großen** Krankenhaus **mit netten** Kollegen." en="She works in a big hospital with nice colleagues." />

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="I live in **a small** town." de="Ich wohne in **einer kleinen** Stadt." note="Both the article and the adjective change after the dative preposition in." />

<Compare en="a house **with a big** garden / a house **with big** gardens" de="ein Haus **mit großem** Garten" note="Without an article German puts the dative ending -em on the adjective." />

<Compare en="**the young woman** likes the jacket" de="die Jacke gefällt **der jungen Frau**" note="English 'woman' is the subject. In German she is the dative and the adjective takes -en." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="Ich wohne in einer **kleine** Stadt." right="Ich wohne in einer **kleinen** Stadt." why="After a dative article the adjective is always -en." />

<Mistake wrong="Er fährt mit dem **neuem** Auto." right="Er fährt mit dem **neuen** Auto." why="dem already shows the -m. The adjective takes -en." />

<Mistake wrong="Ein Haus mit **großen** Garten." right="Ein Haus mit **großem** Garten." why="Without an article, the adjective shows the dative itself: -em for masculine." />

<Mistake wrong="mit den neuen **Nachbar**" right="mit den neuen **Nachbarn**" why="The dative plural noun takes -n." />

## Summary

<Summary>

| Dative | masculine | feminine | neuter | plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dem / einem | dem neu**en** | der neu**en** | dem neu**en** | den neu**en** …n |
| no article | neu**em** | neu**er** | neu**em** | neu**en** …n |

**After any dative article: -en.**

**The full rule:** after der/die/das, -e in five places (nom. sing., acc. f/n), -en everywhere else. After ein, the adjective shows -er / -es where ein has no ending.

**When unsure while speaking:** -en.

</Summary>
