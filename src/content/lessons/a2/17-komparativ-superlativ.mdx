*Berlin is **bigger** than Munich. The train is **as fast as** the car. Which is **the cheapest**?* Comparing is everywhere: in shops, in travel plans, in the A2 speaking exam when you choose between two options. German comparison works much like English *-er* and *-est*, with one big simplification: German never says *more beautiful*. Every adjective, however long, just adds an ending.

## The idea

| | English | German |
| --- | --- | --- |
| base form | cheap | billig |
| comparative | cheap**er** | billig**er** |
| superlative | (the) cheap**est** | **am** billig**sten** |

Two small words connect the comparison:

- **als** after a comparative: *billiger **als***, cheaper **than**
- **wie** for equality: ***so** billig **wie***, **as** cheap **as**

The superlative after a verb always uses **am … -sten**: *Der Zug ist **am schnellsten**.* Before a noun it behaves like any adjective and takes an ending: *der schnell**ste** Zug*.

## Step by step

<Step n="1" title="The comparative: add -er">

<Example de="Das Fahrrad ist **billiger als** das Auto." en="The bike is cheaper than the car." />
<Example de="Heute ist es **kälter als** gestern." en="It is colder today than yesterday." />
<Example de="Deutsch ist **schwieriger als** Englisch." en="German is more difficult than English." />

Long adjectives work the same way: *interessant → interessanter, schwierig → schwieriger, bequem → bequemer.* There is no German word for *more* in this use.

Adjectives ending in *-el* or *-er* drop an *e*: *dunkel → dunkler, teuer → teurer*.

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="The umlaut">

Many short adjectives with **a, o, u** add an umlaut.

| Base | Comparative | Superlative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| alt | **ä**lter | am **ä**ltesten |
| jung | j**ü**nger | am j**ü**ngsten |
| groß | gr**ö**ßer | am gr**ö**ßten |
| kurz | k**ü**rzer | am k**ü**rzesten |
| lang | l**ä**nger | am l**ä**ngsten |
| warm / kalt | w**ä**rmer / k**ä**lter | am w**ä**rmsten / am k**ä**ltesten |
| arm | **ä**rmer | am **ä**rmsten |

<Example de="Mein Bruder ist **älter als** ich, aber ich bin **größer**." en="My brother is older than me, but I am taller." />

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="The superlative: am … -sten">

After a verb: **am** + adjective + **-sten**.

<Example de="Der ICE ist **am schnellsten**." en="The ICE is the fastest." />
<Example de="Im Juli ist es **am wärmsten**." en="It is warmest in July." />

If the adjective ends in **-d, -t, -s, -ß, -z**, add an **e** to make it pronounceable: *am ältesten, am kürzesten, am heißesten*. Exception: *groß → am größten*.

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="The irregular forms">

Learn these five by heart. They are among the most used words in the language.

| Base | Comparative | Superlative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gut | **besser** | am **besten** |
| viel | **mehr** | am **meisten** |
| gern | **lieber** | am **liebsten** |
| hoch | **höher** | am **höchsten** |
| nah | **näher** | am **nächsten** |

<Example de="Ich trinke gern Tee, aber **lieber** Kaffee, und **am liebsten** Kakao." en="I like tea, but I prefer coffee, and most of all I like cocoa." />
<Example de="Das Essen hier ist **besser als** in der Kantine." en="The food here is better than in the canteen." />

*gern, lieber, am liebsten* is how you say what you **prefer**. It is very useful in the speaking exam.

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="Equal and not equal: so … wie">

| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| **so** groß **wie** | as big as |
| **genauso** groß **wie** | just as big as |
| **nicht so** groß **wie** | not as big as |
| größer **als** | bigger than |

<Example de="Mein Zimmer ist **genauso groß wie** dein Zimmer." en="My room is just as big as your room." />
<Example de="Der Bus ist **nicht so schnell wie** die U-Bahn." en="The bus is not as fast as the underground." />

Learners often mix them up, so remember the pair: **comparative + als**, **base form + wie**.

</Step>

<Step n="6" title="Before a noun: normal adjective endings">

Comparative and superlative forms before a noun take the endings you learned in the last three chapters, added **after** the -er or -st.

<Example de="Ich suche **eine größere** Wohnung." en="I am looking for a bigger flat." />
<Example de="Das ist **der schönste** Tag des Jahres." en="That is the most beautiful day of the year." />
<Example de="Wir nehmen **das billigere** Hotel." en="We will take the cheaper hotel." />

*der / die / das + -ste* is the superlative before a noun, or when the noun is understood: *Welches Hotel ist am billigsten? – Das billigste ist das „Krone“.*

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="**more** interesting" de="interessant**er**" note="German never uses 'mehr' to form a comparative. Every adjective takes -er." />

<Compare en="bigger **than**" de="größer **als**" note="Not 'dann' and not 'wie'. After a comparative it is always als." />

<Compare en="**the** fastest (after a verb)" de="**am** schnellsten" note="The train is the fastest: Der Zug ist am schnellsten." />

<Compare en="I **prefer** coffee." de="Ich trinke **lieber** Kaffee." note="German says 'I drink more gladly'. gern, lieber, am liebsten go with the verb." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="Deutsch ist **mehr schwierig** als Englisch." right="Deutsch ist **schwieriger** als Englisch." why="German adds -er even to long adjectives." />

<Mistake wrong="Er ist größer **wie** ich." right="Er ist größer **als** ich." why="After a comparative, use als. wie goes with so … wie." />

<Mistake wrong="Das ist **gutter** / **am gutesten**." right="Das ist **besser** / **am besten**." why="gut is irregular." />

<Mistake wrong="Der Zug ist **schnellste**." right="Der Zug ist **am schnellsten**." why="After the verb the superlative needs am … -sten (or der schnellste, with the article, when a noun is understood)." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich suche eine **größer** Wohnung." right="Ich suche eine **größere** Wohnung." why="Before a noun the comparative takes an adjective ending as well." />

## Summary

<Summary>

| Base | Comparative | Superlative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| billig | billig**er** | am billig**sten** |
| alt (umlaut, +e) | **ä**lt**er** | am **ä**lt**esten** |
| gut | besser | am besten |
| viel | mehr | am meisten |
| gern | lieber | am liebsten |
| hoch | höher | am höchsten |
| nah | näher | am nächsten |

**Comparing:** größer **als** · (genau)so groß **wie** · nicht so groß **wie**

**Before a noun:** eine größer**e** Wohnung · der schönst**e** Tag

</Summary>
