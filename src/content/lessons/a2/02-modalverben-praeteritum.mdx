*I could not come. I had to work. I wanted to call you.* Excuses, explanations and stories about childhood all need modal verbs in the past. German does not use the Perfekt for this. It uses a short one-word past, and for the modal verbs it is easy to form.

## The idea

German has two past tenses: the **Perfekt** (*ich habe gemacht*), which you know, and the **Präteritum** (*ich machte*), a one-word past used mostly in written stories. In conversation the Präteritum is rare, with three exceptions that everyone uses all the time: **sein** (*war*), **haben** (*hatte*), and the **modal verbs**.

The modal Präteritum follows one recipe for all five verbs:

**take the stem, drop the umlaut, add -te, then the ending.**

*können → konn**te**, müssen → muss**te**, dürfen → durf**te**.*

And as with every form of a modal verb, *ich* and *er* are identical. The sentence bracket stays exactly as at A1: modal in position 2, infinitive at the end.

## Step by step

<Step n="1" title="The forms">

| | können | müssen | wollen | dürfen | sollen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ich | konn**te** | muss**te** | woll**te** | durf**te** | soll**te** |
| du | konn**test** | muss**test** | woll**test** | durf**test** | soll**test** |
| er / sie / es | konn**te** | muss**te** | woll**te** | durf**te** | soll**te** |
| wir | konn**ten** | muss**ten** | woll**ten** | durf**ten** | soll**ten** |
| ihr | konn**tet** | muss**tet** | woll**tet** | durf**tet** | soll**tet** |
| sie / Sie | konn**ten** | muss**ten** | woll**ten** | durf**ten** | soll**ten** |

No umlaut anywhere. The endings are **-te, -test, -te, -ten, -tet, -ten**: the same set as *hatte*.

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="konnte: could, was able to">

<Example de="Ich **konnte** gestern nicht kommen." en="I could not come yesterday." />
<Example de="Mit fünf Jahren **konnte** sie schon lesen." en="At five she could already read." />
<Example de="**Konntest** du den Text verstehen?" en="Were you able to understand the text?" />

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="musste: had to">

<Example de="Ich **musste** bis acht Uhr arbeiten." en="I had to work until eight." />
<Example de="Wir **mussten** lange warten." en="We had to wait a long time." />
<Example de="Er **musste** nicht bezahlen." en="He did not have to pay." />

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="wollte: wanted to">

<Example de="Ich **wollte** dich anrufen, aber ich hatte keine Zeit." en="I wanted to call you, but I had no time." />
<Example de="Als Kind **wollte** ich Pilot werden." en="As a child I wanted to become a pilot." />
<Example de="Was **wolltest** du sagen?" en="What did you want to say?" />

*wollte … aber* is the classic shape of an excuse: what you intended, then what stopped you.

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="durfte: was allowed to">

<Example de="Als Kind **durfte** ich nicht lange fernsehen." en="As a child I was not allowed to watch TV for long." />
<Example de="Wir **durften** im Hotel nicht rauchen." en="We were not allowed to smoke in the hotel." />
<Example de="**Durftest** du allein in die Stadt gehen?" en="Were you allowed to go into town alone?" />

</Step>

<Step n="6" title="sollte: was supposed to">

<Example de="Ich **sollte** um neun Uhr da sein." en="I was supposed to be there at nine." />
<Example de="Du **solltest** mich doch anrufen!" en="You were supposed to call me!" />

*sollte* has a second, very common use, which you will meet in chapter 19: giving advice (*Du solltest mehr schlafen*, you should sleep more).

</Step>

<Step n="7" title="möchten has no past of its own">

The past of *Ich möchte* is **Ich wollte**.

<Example de="Heute **möchte** ich ins Kino gehen. Gestern **wollte** ich ins Kino gehen." en="Today I would like to go to the cinema. Yesterday I wanted to go to the cinema." />

And *mögen* (to like) has the past **mochte**: *Als Kind **mochte** ich keinen Fisch.*

</Step>

<Step n="8" title="Telling a story with all three">

A natural spoken past mixes the Perfekt for actions with *war*, *hatte* and the modal pasts.

<Example de="Gestern **wollte** ich ins Kino gehen." en="Yesterday I wanted to go to the cinema." />
<Example de="Aber ich **musste** lange arbeiten und **war** sehr müde." en="But I had to work late and was very tired." />
<Example de="Deshalb **bin** ich zu Hause **geblieben**." en="So I stayed at home." />
<Example de="Ich **konnte** nicht einmal kochen. Ich **habe** eine Pizza **bestellt**." en="I could not even cook. I ordered a pizza." />

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="I **could** not come." de="Ich **konnte** nicht kommen." note="konnte is the past 'could': it says what was or was not possible. For 'could' as a polite request, German uses könnte with an umlaut (chapter 19)." />

<Compare en="I **had to** work." de="Ich **musste** arbeiten." note="English has no past of 'must' and switches to 'had to'. German simply puts müssen into the past." />

<Compare en="I **was allowed to** go." de="Ich **durfte** gehen." note="Three English words, one German one." />

<Compare en="I wanted **to** call you." de="Ich wollte dich anrufen." note="No 'to' after a German modal, and the infinitive goes to the end." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="Ich **könnte** gestern nicht kommen." right="Ich **konnte** gestern nicht kommen." why="The past has no umlaut. könnte means 'could' in the sense of 'would be able to'." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich **habe** arbeiten **gemusst**." right="Ich **musste** arbeiten." why="Modal verbs go into the Präteritum, not the Perfekt, in everyday German." />

<Mistake wrong="Er **mussten** lange warten." right="Er **musste** lange warten." why="er takes -te with no further ending. -ten belongs to wir and sie." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich wollte **anrufen dich**." right="Ich wollte dich **anrufen**." why="The infinitive stands at the very end, in one piece." />

<Mistake wrong="Gestern ich **möchte** ins Kino gehen." right="Gestern **wollte** ich ins Kino gehen." why="möchte has no past. Use wollte, and keep the verb in second place." />

## Summary

<Summary>

**Recipe:** stem, no umlaut, **-te** + ending.

| | können | müssen | wollen | dürfen | sollen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ich / er | konnte | musste | wollte | durfte | sollte |
| du | konntest | musstest | wolltest | durftest | solltest |
| wir / sie | konnten | mussten | wollten | durften | sollten |
| ihr | konntet | musstet | wolltet | durftet | solltet |

| Form | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| konnte | could, was able to |
| musste | had to |
| wollte | wanted to (also the past of möchte) |
| durfte | was allowed to |
| sollte | was supposed to |

**In conversation:** war, hatte and the modals in the Präteritum; every other verb in the Perfekt.

</Summary>
