*I think that…, I hope that…, she says that…* Half of what people say is about what they think or what someone else said. German does this with **dass**, and it is the second word after *weil* that sends the verb to the end. The A2 exam expects short opinions in the speaking part, and *Ich finde, dass …* is how you begin one.

## The idea

**dass** means *that* in sentences like *I think **that** it is good*. It joins a statement to a verb of thinking, knowing, saying or feeling.

It opens a **subordinate clause**, exactly like *weil*. So you already know the grammar: comma before it, conjugated verb at the very end.

*Ich glaube, **dass** der Film gut **ist**.*

Two things are different from English. English often drops *that* (*I think the film is good*); German can drop *dass*, but then the word order changes back. And English *that* has several jobs (*that man*, *the book that I read*); German **dass**, written with double s, has only this one.

## Step by step

<Step n="1" title="The pattern">

| Main clause | dass | … | verb |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ich glaube, | dass | der Film gut | **ist**. |
| Ich weiß, | dass | du viel Arbeit | **hast**. |
| Sie sagt, | dass | sie morgen | **kommt**. |

<Example de="Ich glaube, **dass** der Film gut **ist**." en="I think that the film is good." />
<Example de="Ich weiß, **dass** du viel Arbeit **hast**." en="I know that you have a lot of work." />
<Example de="Sie sagt, **dass** sie morgen **kommt**." en="She says that she is coming tomorrow." />

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="Giving your opinion">

| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Ich glaube, dass … | I believe that … |
| Ich denke, dass … | I think that … |
| Ich finde, dass … | I find that …, in my view … |
| Ich meine, dass … | I am of the opinion that … |
| Ich bin sicher, dass … | I am sure that … |
| Ich bin nicht sicher, ob … | I am not sure whether … |

<Example de="Ich finde, dass Deutsch nicht so schwer ist." en="I find that German is not that hard." />
<Example de="Ich denke, dass wir mehr Zeit brauchen." en="I think that we need more time." />
<Example de="Ich bin sicher, dass er die Prüfung besteht." en="I am sure that he will pass the exam." />

*finden* is for a personal judgement; *glauben* for something you believe to be true but cannot be sure of; *denken* is neutral.

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="Knowing, hoping, being glad">

<Example de="Ich weiß, dass das nicht einfach ist." en="I know that this is not easy." />
<Example de="Ich hoffe, dass du bald wieder gesund bist." en="I hope that you are well again soon." />
<Example de="Ich freue mich, dass du kommst." en="I am glad that you are coming." />
<Example de="Es tut mir leid, dass ich zu spät bin." en="I am sorry that I am late." />
<Example de="Es ist schade, dass du nicht kommen kannst." en="It is a pity that you cannot come." />
<Example de="Es ist wichtig, dass alle pünktlich sind." en="It is important that everyone is on time." />

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="More than one verb">

The rules from *weil* apply unchanged. The conjugated verb is last.

| Type | Example |
| --- | --- |
| modal | Ich glaube, dass er nicht kommen **kann**. |
| Perfekt | Ich weiß, dass sie in Berlin gewohnt **hat**. |
| separable | Sie sagt, dass der Zug um acht **abfährt**. |
| reflexive | Ich hoffe, dass du dich bald besser **fühlst**. |

<Example de="Ich glaube, dass er nicht kommen **kann**." en="I think that he cannot come." />
<Example de="Ich weiß, dass sie in Berlin gewohnt **hat**." en="I know that she lived in Berlin." />
<Example de="Ich hoffe, dass du dich bald besser **fühlst**." en="I hope that you feel better soon." />

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="Reporting what someone said">

<Example de="Anna sagt: Ich habe keine Zeit." en="Anna says: I have no time." />
<Example de="Anna sagt, **dass sie** keine Zeit **hat**." en="Anna says that she has no time." />

Two things change. The verb moves to the end, and the pronoun shifts to fit the new speaker: *ich* becomes *sie*, and the verb ending follows.

| Direct | Reported |
| --- | --- |
| Tom: Ich komme später. | Tom sagt, dass **er** später **kommt**. |
| Die Lehrerin: Der Test ist am Montag. | Die Lehrerin sagt, dass der Test am Montag **ist**. |
| Meine Eltern: Wir besuchen dich. | Meine Eltern schreiben, dass **sie mich** besuchen. |

</Step>

<Step n="6" title="Leaving dass out">

After verbs of thinking and saying you may drop *dass*. Then the second part is a **main clause** again, with the verb in second place.

<Example de="Ich glaube, **dass** er krank **ist**." en="I think that he is ill." />
<Example de="Ich glaube, er **ist** krank." en="I think he is ill." />

Both are correct. What is never correct is a mixture: *dass* with the verb second, or no *dass* with the verb last.

</Step>

<Step n="7" title="dass or das?">

They sound identical and are written differently.

| Word | Job | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **das** (one s) | the article, or *this / that / which* | you can replace it with *dieses* or *welches* |
| **dass** (two s) | the conjunction *that* | you cannot |

<Example de="**Das** Buch ist gut." en="The book is good." />
<Example de="Ich weiß, **dass das** Buch gut ist." en="I know that the book is good." />

</Step>

<Step n="8" title="Agreeing and disagreeing">

<Example de="Ich finde, dass das stimmt." en="I think that is right." />
<Example de="Ich glaube nicht, dass das richtig ist." en="I do not think that is correct." />
<Example de="Da hast du recht." en="You are right there." />
<Example de="Das sehe ich anders." en="I see that differently." />

To disagree, negate the **first** verb: *Ich glaube **nicht**, dass …* This is the natural way, and the same as English *I don't think that …*

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="I think (that) the film **is** good." de="Ich glaube, dass der Film gut **ist**." note="The verb moves to the end after dass. And German always sets the comma." />

<Compare en="I think he is ill. (no 'that')" de="Ich glaube, er ist krank." note="Both languages can leave the word out. In German the clause then keeps normal word order." />

<Compare en="**that** man / the book **that** I read / I know **that** …" de="**der** Mann **dort** / das Buch, **das** ich lese / Ich weiß, **dass** …" note="English uses one word for three jobs. Only the third is dass." />

<Compare en="I'm sorry **that** I'm late." de="Es tut mir leid, **dass** ich zu spät bin." note="The same structure. After expressions of feeling German also uses dass." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="Ich glaube, dass der Film **ist** gut." right="Ich glaube, dass der Film gut **ist**." why="dass sends the conjugated verb to the end of the clause." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich weiß, **das** du recht hast." right="Ich weiß, **dass** du recht hast." why="The conjunction is written with double s." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich glaube dass er kommt." right="Ich glaube**,** dass er kommt." why="A subordinate clause is always separated by a comma." />

<Mistake wrong="Anna sagt, dass **ich** keine Zeit **habe**. (meaning Anna has no time)" right="Anna sagt, dass **sie** keine Zeit **hat**." why="When you report someone's words, the pronoun changes to match who is speaking now." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich glaube, er krank **ist**." right="Ich glaube, er **ist** krank." why="Without dass the clause is a main clause, so the verb is second." />

## Summary

<Summary>

**…, dass + subject + … + conjugated verb.**

| To | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| give an opinion | Ich glaube / denke / finde, dass … |
| say what you know | Ich weiß, dass … |
| express a hope | Ich hoffe, dass … |
| express a feeling | Ich freue mich, dass … · Es tut mir leid, dass … · Schade, dass … |
| report speech | Er sagt, dass … · Sie schreibt, dass … |
| disagree | Ich glaube nicht, dass … |

**Without dass:** normal word order. *Ich glaube, er **ist** krank.*

**das** = the, this, which. **dass** = that (conjunction).

</Summary>
