Finding a flat is one of the first big tasks for anyone who moves to Germany, Austria or Switzerland, and it has its own vocabulary: *Kaltmiete, Nebenkosten, Kaution, WG*. It also appears regularly in the A2 exam: a reading task with flat adverts, a listening task with a landlord, or a short email asking about a flat. This chapter collects the language, and uses the grammar from earlier chapters along the way: adjective endings for describing flats, two-way prepositions for furniture, and polite questions.

## The idea

A move happens in four stages, and each has its own words:

1. **Searching:** reading adverts, *die Wohnungsanzeige*, and contacting the landlord, *der Vermieter* / *die Vermieterin*.
2. **Viewing and signing:** *die Besichtigung*, *der Mietvertrag*, *die Kaution*.
3. **Moving:** *umziehen*, *ausziehen*, *einziehen*.
4. **Living there:** *die Nachbarn*, *die Hausordnung*, small problems.

The three moving verbs are worth learning together. They are all separable, and all form the Perfekt with **sein**:

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **um**ziehen | to move house | Wir **sind** im Mai **umgezogen**. |
| **aus**ziehen | to move out | Ich **bin** bei meinen Eltern **ausgezogen**. |
| **ein**ziehen | to move in | Wir **sind** in die neue Wohnung **eingezogen**. |

## Step by step

<Step n="1" title="Reading an advert">

German flat adverts are short and full of abbreviations.

| Abbreviation | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 3-Zi.-Whg. | Dreizimmerwohnung: flat with three rooms (not counting kitchen and bathroom) |
| m² / qm | Quadratmeter: square metres |
| KM / Kaltmiete | rent without heating and service charges |
| NK / Nebenkosten | service charges: water, rubbish, cleaning |
| WM / Warmmiete | rent including heating and service charges |
| EG / OG / DG | Erdgeschoss, Obergeschoss, Dachgeschoss: ground, upper, attic floor |
| EBK | Einbauküche: fitted kitchen |
| WG | Wohngemeinschaft: flat share |
| ab sofort / ab 1.6. | available now / from 1 June |

<Example de="Helle **3-Zimmer-Wohnung**, 75 m², 2. OG, Balkon, **EBK**, 850 Euro **kalt** plus 180 Euro **Nebenkosten**." en="Bright three-room flat, 75 m², second floor, balcony, fitted kitchen, 850 euros rent plus 180 euros service charges." />
<Example de="Zimmer in netter **WG** frei, 420 Euro **warm**, **ab sofort**." en="Room free in a nice flat share, 420 euros including bills, available now." />

</Step>

<Step n="2" title="Asking about a flat">

Use indirect questions (chapter 9) and the polite forms (chapter 19).

| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Ist die Wohnung noch frei? | Is the flat still available? |
| Wie hoch sind die Nebenkosten? | How much are the service charges? |
| Wie hoch ist die Kaution? | How much is the deposit? |
| Sind Haustiere erlaubt? | Are pets allowed? |
| Wann könnte ich die Wohnung besichtigen? | When could I view the flat? |
| Ab wann ist die Wohnung frei? | From when is the flat available? |

<Example de="Könnten Sie mir sagen, **ob** die Wohnung noch frei ist?" en="Could you tell me whether the flat is still available?" />
<Example de="Ich interessiere mich für Ihre Wohnung. **Wann könnte ich** sie besichtigen?" en="I am interested in your flat. When could I view it?" />

</Step>

<Step n="3" title="Contract and money">

| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| der Mietvertrag | the tenancy agreement |
| die Miete | the rent |
| die Kaution | the deposit (often three months' rent) |
| der Vermieter / die Vermieterin | the landlord / landlady |
| der Mieter / die Mieterin | the tenant |
| kündigen | to give notice |
| die Kündigungsfrist | the notice period |

<Example de="Die **Kaution** beträgt drei Monatsmieten." en="The deposit is three months' rent." />
<Example de="Ich habe meine alte Wohnung **gekündigt**. Die **Kündigungsfrist** ist drei Monate." en="I have given notice on my old flat. The notice period is three months." />

</Step>

<Step n="4" title="Moving day">

<Example de="Am Samstag **ziehen** wir **um**. Kannst du uns beim Tragen helfen?" en="We are moving on Saturday. Can you help us carry things?" />
<Example de="Wir haben einen Transporter **gemietet**." en="We have rented a van." />
<Example de="Die Kisten **stellen** wir erst mal ins Wohnzimmer." en="We will put the boxes in the living room for now." />

After moving you have to **register** at the local office within two weeks: *sich **anmelden** beim Bürgeramt*. Leaving a town: *sich **abmelden***.

</Step>

<Step n="5" title="The house rules">

Most buildings have a *Hausordnung*. Typical points:

| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| die Ruhezeit (22 bis 6 Uhr) | quiet hours (10 pm to 6 am) |
| den Müll trennen | to separate the rubbish |
| die Treppe putzen | to clean the stairs |
| Fahrräder im Keller abstellen | to leave bikes in the cellar |
| Rauchen verboten | no smoking |

<Example de="Bitte **trennen** Sie den Müll: Papier, Plastik, Bio und Restmüll." en="Please separate the rubbish: paper, plastic, organic and other." />
<Example de="Ab 22 Uhr ist **Ruhezeit**." en="Quiet hours start at 10 pm." />

</Step>

<Step n="6" title="Talking to neighbours">

A friendly note is the usual way to deal with a problem or announce a party.

<Example de="Liebe Nachbarn, am Samstag feiern wir unseren **Einzug**. Es wird vielleicht etwas **laut**. Wir **bitten um** Verständnis!" en="Dear neighbours, on Saturday we are celebrating moving in. It may get a bit loud. Thank you for your understanding!" />
<Example de="Entschuldigung, könnten Sie die Musik bitte etwas **leiser** machen? Es ist schon nach elf." en="Excuse me, could you turn the music down a bit? It is already after eleven." />

</Step>

## Compared with English

<Compare en="a **three-bedroom** flat" de="eine **Dreizimmerwohnung**" note="German counts all rooms except kitchen and bathroom. A German three-room flat usually has two bedrooms and a living room." />

<Compare en="The rent is 800 **including bills**." de="Die Miete ist 800 **warm**." note="warm and kalt describe the rent: with or without heating and service charges." />

<Compare en="I **moved** last year." de="Ich **bin** letztes Jahr **umgezogen**." note="All the moving verbs take sein in the Perfekt." />

<Compare en="to **register** your address" de="sich **anmelden**" note="Registering is required by law in Germany within two weeks of moving." />

## Typical mistakes

<Mistake wrong="Ich **habe** letztes Jahr **umgezogen**." right="Ich **bin** letztes Jahr **umgezogen**." why="umziehen is a change of place, so the Perfekt uses sein." />

<Mistake wrong="Ich **ziehe** morgen. (meaning: I am moving)" right="Ich **ziehe** morgen **um**." why="ziehen alone means 'to pull'. Moving house is umziehen." />

<Mistake wrong="Wie viel ist die **Kaltmiete mit** Nebenkosten?" right="Wie hoch ist die **Warmmiete**? / Wie hoch sind die Nebenkosten?" why="Kaltmiete is without service charges; with them it is the Warmmiete." />

<Mistake wrong="Wir ziehen **in der** neuen Wohnung ein." right="Wir ziehen **in die** neue Wohnung ein." why="einziehen is a movement into somewhere: in + accusative." />
