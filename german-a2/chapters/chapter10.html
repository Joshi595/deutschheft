Chapter 10: Genitive Case - Possessive Case

Master the possessive case and express relationships between nouns

📖 Introduction - The Genitive Case

The genitive case (Genitiv) is the possessive case in German. It shows ownership, belonging, or relationships between nouns. While English uses possessive pronouns ('s) or "of", German changes article endings and sometimes noun endings to indicate genitive.

🔑 What is the Genitive Case?

The genitive case answers the question "Wessen?" (Whose?)

  • English: "The car of the man" or "The man's car"
  • German: "Das Auto des Mannes" (The car of the man)
  • English: "The color of the house"
  • German: "Die Farbe des Hauses" (The color of the house)

📋 Genitive Articles - The 4 Cases Summary

Here's how articles change across all four German cases. Notice genitive is the 4th case:

Case Masculine Feminine Neuter Plural
Nominative (subject) der Mann die Frau das Kind die Leute
Accusative (direct object) den Mann die Frau das Kind die Leute
Dative (indirect object) dem Mann der Frau dem Kind den Leuten
Genitive (possessive) des Mannes der Frau des Kindes der Leute

⚠️ Important Genitive Rules

1. Feminine & Plural Stay the Same: Feminine and plural genitive articles are the same as dative (der/den).

2. Masculine & Neuter Add -S or -ES: Masculine and neuter nouns add an ending to the noun:

  • One syllable noun: Add -ES → des Mannes, des Kindes, des Hauses
  • Multi-syllable noun: Add -S → des Lehrs (usually from Lehrer)

📌 Indefinite Articles - Genitive Forms

The indefinite articles (ein, eine, ein) also change in genitive:

Gender Indefinite Genitive Example
Masculine eines Das Auto eines Mannes (A man's car)
Feminine einer Der Preis einer Frau... (A woman's price - unusual)
Neuter eines Die Farbe eines Hauses (A house's color)
Plural - (No indefinite plural article exists)

🔄 Genitive Prepositions

Certain prepositions always require the genitive case. These are important to memorize:

Preposition Meaning Example
wegen because of / due to wegen des Wetters (because of the weather)
trotz despite / in spite of trotz des Regens (despite the rain)
während during während des Unterrichts (during the lesson)
statt / anstatt instead of statt des Autos (instead of the car)
innerhalb within / inside innerhalb der Stadt (within the city)
außerhalb outside / beyond außerhalb des Hauses (outside the house)
oberhalb above oberhalb des Berges (above the mountain)
unterhalb below unterhalb des Berges (below the mountain)

💡 Common Genitive Constructions

1. Simple Possession:

  • "Das Buch des Schülers" (The student's book)
  • "Die Farbe des Autos" (The color of the car)

2. Family Relationships:

  • "Der Vater meines Freundes" (My friend's father)
  • "Die Mutter des Kindes" (The child's mother)

3. With Genitive Prepositions:

  • "Wegen des Regens bleibe ich zu Hause" (Because of the rain, I stay home)
  • "Trotz der Schwierigkeit schaffe ich das" (Despite the difficulty, I can do it)
