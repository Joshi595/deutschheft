Chapter 6: Adjective Endings (Accusative Case)

Master German adjective agreement in the accusative case - the direct object

📖 Introduction

The accusative case marks the direct object of a sentence (who or what is being acted upon). When adjectives modify accusative nouns, their endings change compared to the nominative case.

  • Masculine nouns: Article changes (der → den), and adjective adds -EN
  • Feminine & Neuter nouns: No article change, adjective stays -E
  • Plural: Article changes (die → die), adjective adds -EN

🎯 Key Differences: Nominative vs Accusative

Nominative: "Der große Mann" (The tall man - subject)

Accusative: "Ich sehe den großen Mann" (I see the tall man - object)

✓ Essential: Accusative masculine is the most common case change. Other genders barely change!

📝 Common Accusative Adjectives

German (Nom → Acc) English Example
groß → großen (m.) big/tall Ich kaufe einen großen Laptop
schön → schöne (f.) beautiful Ich habe eine schöne Katze
klein → kleine (n.) small Ich sehe das kleine Kind
alt → alten (m.) old Wir fahren einen alten Auto
neu → neuen (m.) new Ich kaufe einen neuen Hut
schnell → schnellen (m.) fast Er fährt einen schnellen Wagen
intelligent → intelligenten (m.) intelligent Ich sehe einen intelligenten Jungen
freundlich → freundlichen (m.) friendly Ich kenne einen freundlichen Mann
