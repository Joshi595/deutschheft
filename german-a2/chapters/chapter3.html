Chapter 3: Daily Activities & Routines

Learn to describe your daily schedule and what you do every day in German

📖 Introduction

In this chapter, you'll learn vocabulary for common daily activities and how to talk about your daily routine. Being able to describe what you do every day is essential for casual conversation and making new friends in German-speaking countries.

Grammar Point 1: Present Tense Regular Verbs

Regular verbs (weak verbs) follow a predictable pattern in the present tense. They are called "weak verbs" because the stem doesn't change.

Pattern for regular verbs (e.g., "machen" - to do/make):

ich mache (I do)
du machst (you do)
er/sie/es macht (he/she/it does)
wir machen (we do)
ihr macht (you all do)
sie/Sie machen (they/you formal do)

Grammar Point 2: Time Expressions for Daily Routines

Use these time expressions to talk about when you do your activities:

Parts of the day:
• morgens - in the morning
• mittags - at noon
• nachmittags - in the afternoon
• abends - in the evening
• nachts - at night

Frequency:
• jeden Tag - every day
• jede Woche - every week
• immer - always
• meistens - usually
• manchmal - sometimes
• selten - rarely
• nie/niemals - never

Grammar Point 3: Word Order with Time Expressions

In German, adverbs of time usually come early in the sentence, often after the conjugated verb:

Pattern: Conjugated verb + time expression + activity

Example 1:
Ich stehe morgens um 7 Uhr auf.
(I get up in the morning at 7 o'clock.)

Example 2:
Sie arbeitet nachmittags im Büro.
(She works in the afternoon in the office.)

Example 3:
Wir essen abends zusammen.
(We eat in the evening together.)

🎯 Sample Daily Routine (Ein typischer Tag)

A typical day in German:

Ich stehe um 7 Uhr auf. (I get up at 7 o'clock.)
Ich wasche mich und ziehe mich an. (I wash and get dressed.)
Ich frühstücke um 7:30 Uhr. (I have breakfast at 7:30.)
Ich fahre um 8 Uhr zur Arbeit. (I drive to work at 8 o'clock.)
Ich arbeite von 9 bis 17 Uhr. (I work from 9 to 5 pm.)
Ich esse um 12 Uhr Mittagessen. (I eat lunch at noon.)
Ich gehe nach der Arbeit spazieren. (I go for a walk after work.)
Ich koche abends. (I cook in the evening.)
Ich schaue Fernsehen. (I watch TV.)
Ich gehe um 22 Uhr ins Bett. (I go to bed at 10 pm.)
