Chapter 1: Character Traits (Charaktereigenschaften)

Learn how to describe people's personality and character traits in German

📖 Introduction

In this chapter, you will learn how to describe people's character traits and personalities in German. This is essential for everyday conversations when talking about yourself, your friends, family members, or colleagues.

Why Learn Character Traits?

✓ Describe people you know

✓ Understand how others describe people

✓ Build personality vocabulary

✓ Have meaningful conversations about emotions and characteristics

Grammar Point 1: Adjective Agreement

In German, adjectives must agree with the noun they describe in gender, number, and case.

Nominative Case (Subject):
• Der Mann ist freundlich. (The man is friendly.)
• Die Frau ist freundliche. (The woman is friendly.)
• Das Kind ist freundliches. (The child is friendly.)

Grammar Point 2: Using "sein" (to be)

To describe character traits, use the verb "sein" (to be):

Ich bin I am
Du bist You are (informal)
Er/Sie/Es ist He/She/It is
Wir sind We are
Ihr seid You all are (informal)
Sie sind / sie sind You are (formal) / They are

Grammar Point 3: Intensifiers

Use these words to make descriptions more emphatic:

sehr (very) - sehr freundlich = very friendly
ziemlich (quite, rather) - ziemlich intelligent
besonders (especially) - besonders fleißig
viel zu (way too) - viel zu faul = way too lazy
nicht sehr (not very) - nicht sehr humorvoll

Grammar Point 4: Opposite Traits

Learn how to express opposite qualities:

Positive:
• fleißig (hard-working)
• mutig (brave)
• freundlich (friendly)
Negative:
• faul (lazy)
• feige (cowardly)
• ungfreundlich (unfriendly)

Grammar Point 5: Common Question

Wie ist er/sie/es? (What is he/she/it like?)

Answer: Er/Sie ist... (He/She is...)
