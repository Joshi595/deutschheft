📚 Chapter 9: Comparative & Superlative Adjectives

📖 Introduction - Comparing Things in German

Comparative and superlative forms allow you to compare adjectives and express different degrees of qualities. German has three levels: positive (base), comparative (more), and superlative (most).

🔑 Three Degrees of Adjectives

Positive: The base form (just the adjective)

Comparative: Comparing two things (-ER ending + "als" for "than")

Superlative: Comparing three or more things (-EST ending with article + declension)

  • Positive: schnell (fast)
  • Comparative: schneller als (faster than)
  • Superlative: der/die/das schnellste (the fastest)

⚡ Regular Comparative & Superlative Formation

Most German adjectives follow this simple pattern for comparative and superlative:

Positive Comparative Superlative Meaning
klein kleiner der kleinste small, smaller, smallest
groß größer der größte big, bigger, biggest
schnell schneller der schnellste fast, faster, fastest
teuer teurer der teuerste expensive, more expensive, most expensive

📌 Key Rule: Umlaut Changes

Short adjectives with A, O, U vowels add an umlaut in comparative and superlative:

  • alt → älter → der älteste (old, older, oldest)
  • groß → größer → der größte (big, bigger, biggest)
  • kurz → kürzer → der kürzeste (short, shorter, shortest)
  • jung → jünger → der jüngste (young, younger, youngest)

🎯 Irregular Comparative & Superlative Forms

Some common adjectives have irregular comparative and superlative forms:

Positive Comparative Superlative
gut (good) besser der beste
viel (much) mehr der meiste
wenig (little) weniger der wenigste
hoch (high) höher der höchste
nah (near) näher der nächste

💡 Using Comparative in Sentences

Structure: Adjective + -ER + "als" (than)

  • "Mein Auto ist schneller als dein Auto." (My car is faster than your car.)
  • "Sie ist älter als ich." (She is older than me.)
  • "Dieser Film ist interessanter als jener Film." (This film is more interesting than that film.)

Important: Comparative adjectives do NOT need to match gender/case endings when used predicatively (after "sein").

💡 Using Superlative in Sentences

Structure: Definite article (der/die/das) + adjective + -ST(E)

  • "Das ist das schnellste Auto." (That is the fastest car.)
  • "Sie ist die älteste Person hier." (She is the oldest person here.)
  • "Der beste Film ist jener dort." (The best film is that one over there.)

Important: Superlative adjectives MUST match gender and case with their noun. Think of them like regular adjectives with -ste ending.

📝 Common Comparatives & Superlatives

German (Positive) Comparative Superlative English
schön schöner der schönste beautiful, more beautiful, most beautiful
intelligent intelligenter der intelligenteste intelligent, more intelligent, most intelligent
billig billiger der billigste cheap, cheaper, cheapest
neu neuer der neueste new, newer, newest
interessant interessanter der interessanteste interesting, more interesting, most interesting
